# Kabyle vs. confusable languages

Kabyle is not part of any CLD2 close-set, but it is confusable with other Berber (Amazigh) varieties and, superficially, with transliterated North African Arabic.

## Kabyle (kab) vs. Central Atlas Tamazight (tzm) / Tachelhit (shi)

- All three Berber Latin orthographies share **ɛ ɣ ḥ ḍ ṛ ṣ ṭ ẓ**. Kabyle is distinctive for:
  - Heavy geminate onsets **tt-**, **mm-**, **nn-**, **ss-** and **yett-/tett-** imperfective prefixes. Tachelhit uses **tt-** similarly but has fewer geminate onsets; Central Atlas uses **tt-** but also **ar** for imperfective.
  - Kabyle uses **ad** for future; Tachelhit uses **rad**; Central Atlas often uses **rad-** too.
  - Lexical markers: *taqbaylit*, *tamurt*, *taddart*, *aqcic* ("boy") are Kabyle; Tachelhit *tamazirt*, *tarwa*, *afrux*.

## Kabyle vs. Maghrebi Arabic (ary, arz, aeb) in Latin transliteration

- Arabic chat/transliteration uses digits **2 3 5 7 9** for pharyngeals; Kabyle uses proper Unicode **ɛ, ḥ, ɣ, q** instead.
- Kabyle function words **d, n, ur, ara, ad, ɣef, ɣer** are absent in Arabic transliteration, which has **fi, min, ila, w, l-**.

## Kabyle vs. Tifinagh Berber

- If the script is Tifinagh (⵰ ⴰ ⴱ ⵣ), it is Berber but not Latin Kabyle; Kabyle online is overwhelmingly Latin.

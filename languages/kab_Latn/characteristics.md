# Kabyle LID characteristics

## Script signals

Kabyle's Latin orthography is extremely distinctive. Strong signals:

- **ɛ** (U+025B) and **ɣ** (U+0263) — a small-set Afro-Asiatic Latin alphabet; outside Berber-family languages these are very rare.
- Dotted-below letters **ḥ ḍ ṛ ṣ ṭ ẓ** (U+1E25, U+1E0D, U+1E5B, U+1E63, U+1E6D, U+1E93).
- Initial **a-**, **ta-**, **i-**, **ti-**, **u-**, **wu-** on almost every noun.
- Frequent word-initial geminate digraphs **tt-**, **nn-**, **mm-**, **ss-**, **ll-**, **ww-**, reflecting historical assimilation.

## Top function words

**d**, **n**, **i**, **ur**, **ara**, **ad**, **ass**, **deg**, **ɣef**, **ɣer**, **seg**, **s**, **yal**, **kan**, **akken**, **mi**, **ma**, **ma yella**, **neɣ**, **akka**, **nekk**, **nekkini**, **kečč**, **kemm**, **netta**, **nettat**, **nekkni**, **kunwi**, **nutni**, **tura**, **mazal**, **daɣen**, **amzun**, **am**, **armi**, **imi**, **acḥal**, **ayen**, **wigi**, **wigad**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Word-initial sequences `tt-`, `mm-`, `nn-`, `ss-` are very common (*ttqel*, *ttwabeddel*, *mmeslay*, *nnig*).
- Feminine bracket **t-…-t** gives many words starting in `ta-`, `ti-`, `tu-` and ending in `-t`: *taqbaylit*, *tameṭṭut*, *tutlayt*.
- Masculine nouns start in **a-** (*argaz*, *amdan*, *asebter*).
- Genitive linker **n** stands alone between nouns: *asebter n tuɣdadt*, *taddart n tmurt*.
- `-ɣ` at word end signals 1sg verb agreement (*nniɣ*, *uɣeɣ*).
- `yett-`, `tett-`, `nett-` prefixes mark imperfective verbs (*yettwabeddel*, *tettmeslay*).

## Morphology hooks

- Preverbal **ad** + aorist (*ad-uɣaleɣ* "I will return").
- Discontinuous negation **ur … ara** around the verb.
- Dative/accusative clitic chain on verbs: `-as`, `-asen`, `-ak`, `-am`, `-i`, `-iyi`, `-aɣ`.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Standard Latin punctuation. Dates use the Berber calendar (e.g. *ass n 7 Wamber 2020*). Some writers substitute ASCII `3` for **ɛ** and `gh` for **ɣ** in informal text — worth tolerating in the LID model.

# Tamil characteristics (LID signals)

## Script signals

Tamil uses Unicode block **U+0B80–U+0BFF** exclusively. Any text in this block is Tamil; no other living language uses it. Near-certain single-character diagnostics:

- **ழ** (U+0BB4) — retroflex approximant, unique to Tamil among widely-used scripts; appears in the language's own name *தமிழ்*.
- **ற** (U+0BB1) alveolar trill and **ன** (U+0BA9) alveolar nasal — both distinguish Tamil from Sanskrit-based Indic scripts that only have ர and ந.
- **ள** (U+0BB3) retroflex lateral and **ண** (U+0BA3) retroflex nasal in combination with the above.
- Virama **்** (U+0BCD, *puḷḷi*) appears on nearly every consonant cluster and word-final consonant — visible as a dot above the letter.

Absence of aspirated/voiced letter pairs (no க vs கக distinction in writing for /k/ vs /g/) is itself diagnostic versus Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam.

## Top function words

Articles are absent; determiners, pronouns and particles carry the weight: **இது** (this), **அது** (that), **எது** (which), **நான்** (I), **நீ** (you sg.), **அவன்/அவள்/அவர்** (he/she/he-polite), **நாம்/நாங்கள்** (we incl./excl.), **நீங்கள்** (you pl./polite), **இங்கே/அங்கே** (here/there), **என்ன** (what), **ஏன்** (why), **எப்படி** (how), **ஆம்** (yes), **இல்லை** (no/not), **ஆனால்** (but), **அல்லது** (or), **மற்றும்** (and), **என்று** (that, quotative), **போல** (like), **வரை** (until), **பின்** (after), **முன்** (before), **தான்** (emphatic), **உடன்** (with), **ஆக** (as/becoming).

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Word-final **`ன்`**, **`ம்`**, **`ர்`**, **`ள்`** (with visible puḷḷi) are extremely frequent.
- Sequences **`க்கு`**, **`த்து`**, **`ட்டு`**, **`ற்று`** (geminate + உ) mark dative/locative/past forms.
- **`-ின`**, **`-ஆக`**, **`-ஆன`** as common adjectivizer/adverbializer tails.
- Quotative **`என்று`** or colloquial **`-னு`** embedded before reporting verbs.

## Morphology hooks

- Plural **`-கள்`**; dative **`-க்கு/-உக்கு`**; accusative **`-ஐ`**; possessive **`-உடைய/-இன்`**; locative **`-இல்`**.
- Finite verb tails: `-கிறேன்/-கிறான்/-கிறது` (present), `-ந்தேன்/-னான்` (past), `-வேன்/-வான்` (future).
- Verbal noun **`-வது`**, infinitive **`-க்க`**, participle **`-து/-ந்த`**.

## Punctuation / orthography

Standard Latin-style punctuation (. , ? !) is used. Tamil numerals (௦-௯) and special signs (௰ ௱ ௲) exist but are rare in modern text; Western digits are typical. There is no Devanagari-style daṇḍa.

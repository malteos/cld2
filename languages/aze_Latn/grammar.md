# Azerbaijani (macro) grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV, modifier–head ordering throughout.
  Free-ish order because of rich case marking.
- **Morphology**: Agglutinative with long, transparent suffix
  chains. Nine-vowel harmony (including `ə`) controls allomorphy:
  front/back plus rounded/unrounded, with `ə` patterning as a front
  unrounded low vowel.
- **Nouns**: No gender, no article. Six cases: nominative (∅),
  accusative *-(n)ı/-(n)i/-(n)u/-(n)ü*, dative *-a/-ə*, locative
  *-da/-də*, ablative *-dan/-dən*, genitive *-ın/-in/-un/-ün*.
  Plural *-lar/-lər*. Possessive suffixes (*-ım/-im/-um/-üm* etc.)
  attach before case.
- **Verbs**: Suffix slots: negation *-ma/-mə*, ability *-(y)a bil*,
  tense/aspect (*-dı/-di/-du/-dü* past, *-mış/-miş* evidential past,
  *-ır/-ir/-ur/-ür* present, *-acaq/-əcək* future, *-ar/-ər*
  aorist), then personal endings. Copula clitics *-dır/-dir/-dur/-dür*.
- **Phonology**: Nine-vowel system `a e ə i ı o ö u ü`. Native
  consonants include /q/ (uvular, written `q`) and /x/ (velar
  fricative, written `x`). Stress is usually final. Word-final
  voiced obstruents are common, in contrast with Turkish devoicing
  patterns.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. No overt "to be" copula in present
  affirmative. Negation is suffixal; polar questions use the clitic
  *mı/mi/mu/mü* or rising intonation. Participial and converbial
  constructions build subordinate clauses.

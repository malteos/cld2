# Southern Uzbek vs confusable languages

## uzs vs Persian (fas)

- Vowel letters **ۉ** (U+06C9) and **ې** (U+06D0), velar nasal **ڭ** (U+06AD) → uzs, not fas.
- Turkic suffix chains **ـلر، ـده، ـدن، ـگه، ـنینگ، ـدی، ـگن** → uzs. Persian uses **ـها** plural and Indo-European-style verbal endings.
- uzs lacks Persian *ezāfe* kasra **ـِ** and the object marker **را**.
- uzs drops the copula in the present tense; fas uses clitic copula **ـم، ـی، ـست**.

## uzs vs Urdu (urd)

- urd has retroflexes **ٹ ڈ ڑ** and aspirate **ھ** digraphs (بھ، تھ، کھ). uzs does not use them.
- urd sentence terminator **۔** (U+06D4); uzs uses `.`.
- urd genitive postpositions **کا/کی/کے** and copula **ہے/ہیں** are Indo-Aryan and absent from uzs.
- uzs vowel letters **ۉ ې** and velar nasal **ڭ** do not occur in Urdu.

## uzs vs Northern Uzbek (uzn)

- Northern Uzbek (`uzn_Latn` / `uzn_Cyrl`) is script-disjoint from `uzs_Arab`; any Arabic-block text versus Latin/Cyrillic is decisive.
- Vocabulary and grammar are very close — the two are often mutually intelligible — but CommonLID separates them by script and region.

## uzs vs Kazakh / Kyrgyz / Uyghur

Kazakh and Kyrgyz in Arabic script use letters like **ۇ، ۋ، ى** differently; within CommonLID they are usually Cyrillic-scripted and thus trivially distinct. Uyghur (`uig_Arab`) is the closest Arabic-script Turkic relative but uses a different and more standardised vowel-letter inventory (**ۇ، ۆ، ۈ، ې، ى**) and vowel-explicit spelling of every syllable, whereas uzs still tolerates Persian-style unmarked short vowels.

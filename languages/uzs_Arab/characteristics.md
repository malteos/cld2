# Southern Uzbek LID characteristics

## Script signals

- Persian extensions **پ چ ژ گ** all appear; in addition, diagnostic Turkic vowel letters:
  - **ۉ** (U+06C9) for /ø, ɵ/
  - **ې** (U+06D0) for /e/
  - Sometimes **ۆ** (U+06C6) and **ۈ** (U+06C8).
- **Absence** of retroflex letters **ٹ ڈ ڑ** (those signal urd).
- Absence of the Persian *ezāfe* kasra **ـِ**.
- Velar nasal **ڭ** (U+06AD) or digraph **نگ** spelling /ŋ/, which does not occur in Persian or Urdu.

## Top function words

و (and, Persian loan), هم (also), بیر (one), بو (this), شو (that), هر (every), کیم (who), نیمه (what), قچان (when), قنده (how), قيله (so/thus), بیلن (with), اوچون (for), کبی (like), ایسه (if), ایکن (that/evidential), دیب (saying/that), یوق (no/not), ها (yes), امّا (but), لکن (but).

## N-gram and spelling patterns

- Suffix chains are visible on the surface: **ـلر** (plural), **ـده / ـته** (locative), **ـدن / ـتن** (ablative), **ـنینگ / ـنیڭ** (genitive), **ـگه / ـقه** (dative), **ـنی** (accusative), **ـدی** (past), **ـگن** (perfect), **ـیب** (converb).
- Bigram **لر** at or near end of words is highly diagnostic (plural).
- Final **ـدیر / ـدور** (copula/emphatic) in formal register.
- Back-vowel / front-vowel doublets in the same text (e.g. both **ـده** and **ـته**, both **ۉ** and **و**) — a marker of Turkic vowel-harmony orthography.

## Morphology hooks

- Agglutinative suffix stacking, e.g. **کتابلریمیزده** *kitāb-lar-imiz-da* "in our books".
- Verb negation **ـمه / ـمی** inside the verb form rather than as a separate word.
- Evidential **ـمیش / ـگن ایکن** suffixes.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

- Right-to-left. Full stop is Latin `.` (not Urdu **۔**). Comma **،** and question mark **؟** are Persian/Arabic style.
- ZWNJ sometimes separates stems from Turkic suffixes but less consistently than in Persian.

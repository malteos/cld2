# Standard Latvian grammar

- **Word order**: SVO default; relatively free owing to case marking.
  Fusional and synthetic. Stress is fixed on the first syllable.
- **Morphology**: Fusional, heavily suffixing. Productive derivational
  affixes: `-ība, -ums, -šana` (abstract nouns), `-nieks, -tājs, -ējs`
  (agents), `-īgs, -isks, -ots` (adjectives), `-ot, -āt, -ēt, -īt, -ināt`
  (verb classes).
- **Nouns**: Two genders (masculine, feminine) and two numbers (singular,
  plural — no dual). Seven cases: nominative, genitive, dative, accusative,
  instrumental (syncretic with accusative sg. and dative pl.), locative,
  vocative. Six declensions. Typical m.sg.nom endings **-s, -š, -is, -us**
  (*vīrs, ceļš, brālis, tirgus*); f.sg.nom **-a, -e** (*māte, sieva*). No
  articles; a weak definite/indefinite distinction is expressed in the
  adjective (definite `-ais/-ā/-ie/-ās`, indefinite `-s/-a/-i/-as`).
- **Verbs**: Three conjugation classes (plus irregular **būt** "be", **iet**
  "go"). Three tenses: present, past (sigmatic preterite), future (marked
  by **-š-**/**-s-**: `es runāšu, tu runāsi`). Moods: indicative,
  conditional (`-tu` throughout persons: `runātu`), imperative (2sg = bare
  stem, 1pl/2pl with endings), and distinctively the **oblique** (evidential)
  mood using the present participle in **-ot** (`esot, strādājot`).
  Debitive mood with clitic **jā-** + 3sg (`man jāiet` "I must go").
- **Phonology**: Phonemic vowel length (a/ā, e/ē, i/ī, u/ū). Six or seven
  diphthongs (ai, ei, ie, au, ui, uo — written `o`). Three tonal contours
  on long syllables (level, falling, broken) marginally preserved in
  standard speech. Consonants include palatalised `ģ ķ ļ ņ` and postalveolar
  `č š ž`. Stress: first syllable.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop in 3rd person; subject pronouns usually overt in 1/2.
  Copula `būt` present but omissible. Negation `ne-` prefix
  (`nezinu, nebija, nav`). Reflexive/middle verbs in `-ties` (`mazgāties`).

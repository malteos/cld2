# Standard Latvian LID characteristics

## Script signals

Latin alphabet with macrons plus cedillas. Signature features:
- **Macron vowels ā ē ī ū** combined with **cedilla consonants ģ ķ ļ ņ** and
  **háček consonants č š ž** — this combined set is diagnostic.
- **No y** — distinguishes Standard Latvian from Latgalian.
- **No ą/ę/į/ų/ė** — distinguishes from Lithuanian.
- **No õ, ä, ö, ü** — distinguishes from Estonian/Finnish/German.
- **No ł, ż, ś, ń** — distinguishes from Polish.

## Top function words

un, ir, es, tu, viņš, viņa, viņi, viņas, mēs, jūs, bet, vai, kas, kā, kur,
kad, ko, ka, par, no, uz, pie, ar, bez, pēc, pirms, starp, caur, līdz, jau,

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Obligatory masculine-singular **-s** (or **-š**) ending on indefinite
  nouns and adjectives (`labs vīrs`, `jauns students`).
- Sigmatic future marker **-š-/-s-** before personal endings:
  `būšu, strādāšu, iešu, runāšu`.
- Infinitive in **-t** (`lasīt, runāt, iet, būt`).
- Abstract nouns in **-ība, -ums, -šana**.
- Adjectives in **-īgs, -isks, -ots, -īts**.
- Diphthong digraphs **ai, ei, au, ui, ie**; **o** for /uɔ/ in native words.
- Frequent triple-letter sequence **-ties** on reflexive infinitives
  (`mazgāties, smieties, atrasties`).

## Morphology hooks

- Debitive **jā-** + 3sg: `jāstrādā, jāzina, jāiet, jābrauc`.
- Oblique/evidential -ot participle: `esot, strādājot, lasot`.
- Conditional uniform `-tu` through all persons: `es darītu, viņš darītu`.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

- Quotation marks: outer `„…"` (low-high).
- Dialogue dash `—`.
- Capitalisation as in other Baltic/Slavic norms: months, weekdays,
  nationalities lowercase; place names and proper nouns capitalised.

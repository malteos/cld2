# Distinguishing Moroccan Arabic (ary)

## ary vs MSA (arb / ara)

- Presence of **ديال / د** (genitive particle) and **بزاف** is near-proof of ary.
- Imperfective prefix **كـ/تـ** (كيقول, كتعمل) — MSA has no such prefix.
- Future **غادي / غا** — MSA uses سـ/سوف.
- Negation **ما...ش / ماشي** — MSA uses لا/ما/لم/لن/ليس.
- Demonstratives **هاد / هاديك / هاك** — MSA هذا / هذه / ذلك.
- Heavy French/Spanish/Berber vocabulary (دابا, واخا, شوية, مزيان) absent from MSA.

## ary vs aeb (Tunisian Arabic)

Closest Maghrebi sibling.
- ary imperfective prefix **كـ/تـ**; aeb has **no** such prefix (bare impf is
  present — نكتب = "I/we write", with 1pl disambiguated by ـوا).
- ary future **غادي / غا**; aeb future **باش / بش**.
- ary "what" **شنو / أشنو / أش**; aeb "what" **شنوة / آش / أش**.
- ary "a lot" **بزاف** (also in aeb but less exclusive); aeb specific **برشة**.
- aeb 1sg impf **نـ** with 1pl **نـ...ـوا** (the Maghrebi 1pl marker) — similar
  to ary; but ary adds habitual كـ, aeb does not.

## ary vs arz (Egyptian)

- /q/: ary keeps **[q]**; arz → **[ʔ]**.
- Genitive particle **ديال** (ary) vs **بتاع** (arz).
- ary demonstrative **هاد**; arz **ده / دي / دول**.
- ary future **غادي**; arz **هـ/حـ**.

## ary vs ars (Najdi) / apd (Sudanese)

- ars and apd both realise /q/ as **[g]** and lack **كـ/تـ** habitual prefix
  and **ديال** genitive.
- ars uses **ويش** "what", apd uses **شنو** similar to ary but with **دا/دي**
  demonstratives, unlike ary هاد.
- Neither has **بزاف**, **دابا**, or French loans of ary.

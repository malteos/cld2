# Moroccan Arabic (Darija) grammar

- **Word order**: SVO dominant, with topicalised orders common. Synthetic
  Semitic morphology but with strong reduction of unstressed short vowels
  yielding complex consonant clusters (e.g. *kteb* "he wrote", *nketbu* "we
  write").
- **Morphology**: Root-and-pattern templates preserved. Productive clitics:
  imperfective prefix **كـ / تـ** (kayketb, tayketb "he writes/is writing"),
  future **غادي / غا-** (غادي نمشي "I'm going to go"), negation
  **ما...ش / ماشي**, object-pronoun suffixes ـني، ـك، ـه، ـها، ـنا، ـكم، ـهم.
- **Nouns**: Masc/fem (ة). Broken plurals dominate; sound plurals ـين, ـات.
  Genitive often with analytic particle **ديال / د** ("of"): الكتاب ديالي
  "my book", الدار د محمد "Muhammad's house". No case.
- **Verbs**: Perfective vs imperfective. The **كـ/تـ** prefix marks
  habitual/progressive indicative; bare imperfective is subjunctive/modal.
  Future **غادي** + impf. Negation wraps ma-...-ši: مامشيتش "I didn't go".
- **Phonology**: /q/ preserved as **[q]** (distinct from arz); heavy consonant
  clusters; interdentals ث ذ ظ merged with t/d/ḍ; emphatics expanded beyond
  Classical set (ṛ, ḷ, ḅ, ṃ possible). Short-vowel syncope yields words like
  *ktebt* (I wrote), *tketb* (you write).
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; no present copula. Genitive particle **ديال**
  is a hallmark. Interrogatives: **أشنو / شنو / أش** "what", **فين / فاين**
  "where", **فوقاش / إمتى** "when", **علاش** "why", **كيفاش** "how", **شكون**
  "who". Demonstratives: **هاد** / **هاديك** / **هاك** (proximal/distal).

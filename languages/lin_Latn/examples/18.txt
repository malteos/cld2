Botomboki ebandaki na 11h00 ngonga ya esika wana (UTC+1) na Whitehall na ngambu mosusu bapolisi bazalaki kobatela esika ya kokota na Downing Street, esika premier ministre afandaka. Kaka na nsima ya 11:00, batomboki bakangaki nzela na likalo na nzela ya nord ya Whitehall. Na 11:20, polisi esengaki batomboki bazonga na nzela, eyebisaki bango ete ata baza na lotomo ya kotomboka basengeli kokanga bato nzela ya kotambola te. Pene ya 11:29, batomboki bakendaki kino na Whitehall, balekaki na Trafalgar Square, pene ya Strand, balekaki na Aldwych pe bamataki na Kingsway mpona kokende na ndambo ya Holborn esika parti ya ba conservateur ezalaki kosala likita na bango ya printemps na hotel Grand Connaught Rooms.

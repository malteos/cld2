# Source: https://www.sabc.co.za/sabc/thobela-fm/ (SABC Thobela FM — Sepedi radio)

Under Employment Equity and Management Control, the SABC continues to promote representative leadership structures that mirror South Africa’s racial, gender, and ethnic diversity. This includes steadily increasing the participation of black people, particularly black women, across its Board of Directors, Executive Committee, and top management levels.

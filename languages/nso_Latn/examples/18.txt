Mogwanto o thomile ka bo 11:00 nako ya selegae (UTC+1) ka whitehall go lebana le botseno bjo bo letilwego ke maphodisa go Downing Street, legaeng la semmušo la Tonakgolo. Ka morago ga 11:00, bagwanti ba thibile tsela ya difatanaga go tsela ya lebowa ka Whitehall. Ka 11:20, maphodisa a kgopetše bahlolampherefere gore ba boele morago godimo ga tselanathoko , ba bolela gore ba hloka go lekalekanya tokelo ya go gwanta le tlalelo ya difatanaga yeo e bego e thoma go ba gona. Ka bo 11:29, mogwanto o ile godimo go Whitehall, wa feta Trafalgar Square, kgauswi le Strand, wa feta Aldwych go ya godimo ka Kingsway go lebantša Holborn moo mokgatlo wa Conservative Party o be o swere Foramo ya bona ya Seruthwane hoteleng ya Grand Connaught Rooms.

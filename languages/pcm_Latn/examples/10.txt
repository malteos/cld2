# Source: Wikipedia article "Abba Musa Rimi" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

Pope John Paul II kom Kaduna for February 1982. Bot som pipul wey dey rul di muslim pipul dat taim no gri kom grit am. Wen di Pope stil dey di airport bifo im kom enta plen go bak to Lagos, e rid one tin wey dem rait to Rimi an oda pipu wey dey wok for govament sey mek evribodi dey join bodi to do tins togeda, weda you bi muslim or kristen. For August 1982, Rimi yuz gra-gra tok to di Jama'atul Nasril Islam (JNI) sey mek dem si as dem go fit stop di fight-fight wey dey amog di smol smol muslim grup wey dem nem na Izala an Darika Muslim grup. For March 27 1982, dat taim, Rimi kom stat di IBBI Naija wey stil dey try wel wel to dey du drink for North side for Naija.

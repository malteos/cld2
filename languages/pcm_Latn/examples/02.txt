# Source: Wikipedia article "Abass Akande Obesere" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

Alhaji Obesere moto wey bi Lexus jeep Lx470 get asident for Ijebu wen im dey kom Lagos. Di asident hapun for Sunday April 8 2012, di taim wey di tin hapun na 7:30pm. Pipul wey dey tok niuz tok sey Obesere an two oda pesin dey for di moto wen di tin hapun an no bi sey dem dai as dey kari dem go hospitu for Orisunbare Hospital, Jakande Isolo, Lagos. Na wund di two pesin get for di moto.

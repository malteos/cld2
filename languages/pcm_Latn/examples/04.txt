# Source: Wikipedia article "Abayomi Barber" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

Afta dem giv am di prize, dem kom tel am to draw di oga kpata-kpata for Ikoyi klub wey wan stop to bi di oga kpata-kpata wey im nem na Harold Cooper. Dem giv Barber wok for Legos wen 1952 jost dey stat as pesin wey dey draw for buk an for pipul wey wan mek evribodi sabi wetin dem dey sel for one Naijá Kompini for Yaba insaid Legos. For 1955, Barber join one pipul wey de yuz stone do fain-fain tins for Yaba College of Technology wey di oga kpata-kpata na one man wey kom from Britain wey im nem na Paul Mount.

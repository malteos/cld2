# Source: Wikipedia article "Abayomi Barber" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

For 1960 go rish 1962 di Govament kari am go Central School of Arts and Crafts for London. For dis skul, Barber len how to kip tins mek dem no spoil an e kom stil kontiniu di wok wey dem yuz stone tek mek Obafemi Awolowo. Wahala kom hapun for insaid di Govament wey kom mek govament stop to dey pay for im skul. As im dey for England e kontiniu to dey len how to dey draw, e len how dem dey yuz stone mek fain fain tins for  Mancini an Tozer studios wey dey inside London.

# Source: Wikipedia article "Abayomi Barber" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

How im tek stat life Dem born Barber for October 23, 1928 for Ile-Ife for Samuel Bamidele an Victoria Waleola Barber famili. Na for St Peter's Anglican School e tek stat im skul mata for Iremo wey dey inside Ife an na for 1948 im finish im skul for St Stephen's Skul for inside Modakeke for Ife. For 1949, Barber yuz stik du one fain tin an im kom du plenti kompitishon for insaid dat year.

# Source: Wikipedia article "Abass Akande Obesere" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

Abass Akande Obesere (dem bon am for January 20, 1965)  wey pipul dey kol "Omo Rapala" na Naija Muzishan, singa an rekod produsa wey kom fom Ibadan, wey bi big ples for Naija. Obesere na pesin wey pipul no wel wel for im wok wey bi to dey sing fuji song. Meni pipul laik im wok bikos di kain mentod wey e dey yuz arenj im song dey mek pipul for maket, strit, vilej an garaj laik dem. E dey folo di Sikiru Ayinde Barrister ezampul wey pipul sabi wel wel for Naijá an for di wold, Obesere don kari im muzik go difren difren kontri sef. Sony Music giv am kontrat bot im komot wen dey no gree ontop moni mata, Obesere kon go du kontrat for anoda ples, Obesere an K1 D ultimate wey dey sing fuji muzik sef no dey gree for diasef.

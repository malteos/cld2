# Source: Wikipedia article "Abba Musa Rimi" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

Abba Musa Rimi, CON (dem bon am for 28 February 1940) na one man fom Naija wey pipul chuz mek e bi di pesin wey go rul dem for Kaduna Stet for 1979 wen Naija dey for dia Sekond Ripoblik, dis wan kon mek Rimi bi di pesin wey go dey du laik  gofnor for dat taim wey dem komot Gofnor Abdulkadir Balarabe Musa as di gofnor for June 23, 1981. Na wen im de insaid di pati for politis wey dem dey kol People's Redemption Party na im dem tek chuz am as di gofnor of Kaduna Stet.

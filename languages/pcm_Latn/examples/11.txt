# Source: Wikipedia article "Abba Musa Rimi" (https://pcm.wikipedia.org/)

Afta General Muhammadu Buhari yuz gra-gra bigin rul Naija for  December 31 1983, E arest som of di pipul wey dey rul difren difren Stet for Naija. For March 28 1985, dem send Rimi go 21 years for insaid prisin. Dem sey im kari N500,000 dash 96 pipul for di Stet House of Assembly wey dey wok for ples wey dem dey join bodi rait evritin wey pipul sopos du an di one wey dem no sopos do. Rimi sey im giv dem di moni bikos dem "folo do evritin wey dem sopos du for dia difren difren aria.

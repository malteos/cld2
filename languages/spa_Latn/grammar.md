# Spanish grammar

- **Word order**: SVO default, flexible with rich agreement. Questions are marked by inversion or intonation (written with `¿…?`). Clitic pronouns are proclitic to finite verbs (*lo veo*) and enclitic to infinitives, gerunds, and positive imperatives (*verlo, viéndolo, dímelo*).
- **Morphology**: Fusional. Rich verb inflection; nouns inflect for number and are lexically gendered.
- **Nouns**: Two genders (M/F). Plural in `-s / -es`. Definite articles `el la los las`; indefinite `un una unos unas`. Contractions `al` (a + el), `del` (de + el).
- **Adjectives**: Agree in gender and number; usually postnominal. A few preposed adjectives apocopate (*buen, gran, primer, tercer, San*).
- **Verbs**: Three conjugations (`-ar -er -ir`). Full TAM: present, imperfect, preterite, pluperfect, future, conditional, present/imperfect subjunctive, imperative. Compound tenses use `haber` + participle (one auxiliary only). Distinctive indicative/subjunctive contrast used in complement and adverbial clauses. Two copulas `ser` vs. `estar`.
- **Pronouns**: Pro-drop; subject pronouns used for emphasis or clarification. Distinctive object clitic doubling (*a mí me gusta*). Polite `usted/ustedes`. Voseo (*vos tenés*) in much of the Southern Cone and Central America.
- **Phonology**: Five-vowel system `a e i o u`; no vowel reduction. Distinctive trill `/r/` vs. tap `/ɾ/` written `rr` vs. `r`. `j g(e,i)` = /x/; `ñ` = /ɲ/; `ll` = /ʎ~ʝ/.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop, preverbal negation `no`, obligatory subjunctive in many clause types, differential object marking with `a` for animate direct objects.

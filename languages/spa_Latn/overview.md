# Spanish (spa)

## Summary

Spanish (endonym: *español* or *castellano*) is a Romance language (Indo-European → Italic → Romance → Ibero-Romance → West Iberian). ISO codes: 639-1 `es`, 639-2 `spa`, 639-3 `spa`. Written in the Latin script. Native speakers: ~485 million, total speakers ~560 million — the second most-spoken native language in the world. Official in Spain and 19 countries in the Americas (Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Peru, Venezuela, Chile, Cuba, etc.) plus Equatorial Guinea. Co-official with English in Puerto Rico.

## Writing system

Uses the 27-letter Spanish Latin alphabet including `ñ`. Letters `k w` appear only in loanwords. Diacritics are the acute accent `á é í ó ú` (marking irregular stress) and diaeresis `ü` in `güe güi` (to mark `u` pronunciation). The tilde on `ñ` is a graphemic part of the letter, not a diacritic. Uses inverted `¿` and `¡` at the start of interrogative/exclamatory sentences. Standard bicameral casing; Spanish-style quotation `« »` or `" "`.

## Status

Regulated by the Real Academia Española in cooperation with 22 national academies (ASALE). Two major norms coexist: Peninsular Spanish (distinguishing `/θ/` vs `/s/`) and Latin-American Spanish (seseo, yeísmo). Official language of the UN, EU, Mercosur, OAS.

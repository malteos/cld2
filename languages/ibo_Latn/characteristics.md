# Igbo LID characteristics

## Script signals

Igbo is identified primarily by its **under-dot vowels**:

- **ị** (U+1ECB / U+1ECA), **ọ** (U+1ECD / U+1ECC), **ụ** (U+1EE5 / U+1EE4) — the combination of under-dots on **i, o, u** is near-exclusive to Igbo. (Yoruba uses under-dot on **e, o, s** — disjoint sets.)
- Digraphs at high density: **gb, kp, gh, kw, gw, nw, ny, ch, sh** (*gburugburu, kpakpando, nwoke, nyere, gharịa, kwụọ*).
- Syllabic **m** or **ṁ/ṅ** (U+1E41, U+1E45) at word onset: *m ga-eme, n'ụlọ, ṁbe*.
- Apostrophe **'** used as a short form of prepositions **na**, **nke** before vowels: *n'ụlọ, n'ihi, n'etiti, n'ala*.

## Top function words

**na**, **ka**, **bụ**, **dị**, **nke**, **ma**, **mana**, **niile**, **mmadụ**, **ihe**, **m**, **gị**, **anyị**, **unu**, **ha**, **ọ**, **ya**, **ndị**, **ga-**, **ga eme**, **na-**, **gaa**, **dị ka**, **ebe**, **mgbe**, **otu**, **abụọ**, **atọ**, **anọ**, **ise**, **ụmụ**, **ezi**, **ọzọ**, **naanị**, **kama**, **iji**, **n'ihi**, **n'etiti**, **n'ime**, **ị**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Initial vowel on most nouns (*ụlọ, ala, ihe, oche, akwụkwọ, onye*).
- Contracted preposition **n'-** before vowel: *n'ihi, n'ime, n'ụlọ, n'ala* — highly diagnostic.
- Progressive **na-** prefix on verbs: *na-eme, na-ekwu, na-abịa, na-acha*.
- Future **ga-** prefix on verbs: *ga-abịa, ga-eme, ga-agụ*.
- Negative suffix **-ghị / -ghi** at end of verb: *emeghị, amaghị, abụghị*.
- Reduplicated/compound forms with **-kwa**, **-la**, **-ra/-re**, **-wara/-were**.

## Morphology hooks

- Copula contrast **bụ** vs. **dị** is a single-word cue.
- Plural marker **ndị** before a noun phrase.
- Preverbal clitics **m / ị / ọ / anyị / unu / ha** at sentence start.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Apostrophe contractions are load-bearing (*n'ụlọ* ≠ *n ụlọ*). Many online sources omit tone marks entirely; under-dots are usually kept because they are contrastive. Some older texts use **ṅ** for syllabic nasal; modern corpora often substitute plain **n**.

Endeavour Mining bụ ụlọ ọrụ na-egwupụta ihe n'ọtụtụ mba nke nwere ma na-arụ ọrụ na Côte d'Ivoire, Burkina Faso na Senegal. Ụlọ ọrụ ahụ bụ isi na London, England, ma gafere na London Stock Exchange na United Kingdom, Toronto Stock Exchange na Canada, na Over-The-Counter na United States. Ọ bụ akụkụ nke FTSE 100 Index.

# Source: Wikipedia article "Akwụkwọ Nsọ" (https://ig.wikipedia.org/)

Onye edemede-onye nduzi-onye na-emepụta James Cameron nyere edemede dị peeji 57 nke e nyere ya nkwekọrịta ide n'oge mmepe nke ihe nkiri Spider-Man (2002) mbụ, nke ọ ga-eduzi. Mgbe Cameron hapụrụ ọrụ ahụ, onye na-ede ihe nkiri David Koepp gbasaa ya n'ime edemede mbụ, nke ndị edemede ndị ọzọ na-amaghị aha ha mechara rụọ ọrụ.
 N'afọ 2005, Sony Pictures kwụrụ onye edemede Ken Nolan US $ 3 nde maka edemede ya dị peeji 75 nke bụ mgbanwe nke akwụkwọ akụkọ Whitley Strieber a na-ebipụtaghị n'oge ahụ The Grays . Nolan nwere naanị otu ihe odide n'oge ahụ, ihe nkiri maka ihe nkiri ndị agha Black Hawk Down, ọrụ nke o nyefere edemede atọ dị iche iche na onye na-emepụta Jerry Bruckheimer na onye na'ime ndị na-emepụta Mike Stenson, na Chad Oman maka nkwado n'oge usoro ederede.

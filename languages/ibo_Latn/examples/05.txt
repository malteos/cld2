# Source: Wikipedia article "Akwụkwọ Nsọ" (https://ig.wikipedia.org/)

Maka ihe nkiri Batman The Dark Knight (2008), onye edemede David S. Goyer na onye nduzi ihe nkiri ahụ Christopher Nolan dere edemede nke Nolan na nwanne ya nwoke Jonathan Nolan jiri mee ihe iji gbasaa n'ime ihe nkiri a rụchara. "M dere ihe ị ga-akpọ 'ihe odide' na Chris n'ime oge dị ogologo, mgbe ahụ anyị nyefere ya nwanne ya nwoke [Jonathan], onye mere nke mbụ", ka Goyer kọwara.

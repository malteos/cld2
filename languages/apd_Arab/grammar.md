# Sudanese Arabic grammar

- **Word order**: SVO dominant. Synthetic Semitic morphology with simplified
  clitic system compared to MSA but more conservative than Egyptian.
- **Morphology**: Root-and-pattern Arabic. Preserves interdentals ث ذ ظ (unlike
  arz/ary). Indefinite noun + adjective linker **ـن** occurs (relic of
  tanwīn): *beetin kabīr* "a big house". Clitic object suffixes, no case marking.
- **Nouns**: Masc/fem (ة). Broken plurals dominant. Genitive by construct or
  with particle **بتاع / حق** ("of") in colloquial: الكتاب بتاعي / حقي.
- **Verbs**: Perfective vs imperfective. Imperfective **without** habitual
  prefix (unlike arz بـ and ary كـ) — bare impf = indicative present. Future
  **حـ / ح-** + impf, and **بـ** is available as in Egyptian but less
  frequent. Negation **ما** + verb (often without ـش, contrasting with arz),
  and **ما + ش** variant also attested regionally.
- **Phonology**: /q/ → **[g]** (قال → *gāl*). /dʒ/ preserved as [dʒ] (unlike
  Egyptian [g]). Interdentals ث ذ ظ preserved. Classical /f/ sometimes
  realised [b]. Characteristic long-vowel lengthening and stress patterns.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; no present copula. Demonstratives **دا / دي /
  ديل** (proximal; overlap with Egyptian ده / دي / دول). Interrogatives:
  **شنو** (what — Sudanese hallmark), **وين** (where), **متين / امتى** (when),
  **ليه / مالك** (why), **كيف** (how), **منو / مين** (who). Intensifier
  **شديد / ساي / بس** and frequent use of **زول** ("person" — very diagnostic).

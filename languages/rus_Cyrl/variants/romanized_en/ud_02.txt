K 1985 godu razlichnymi vidami obucheniya zdes bylo okhvacheno okolo 1,3 milliona chelovek. Primerno stolko zhe detey obuchalos i k 1991 godu v 1307 nachalnoy i sredney shkolakh. S 2001/ 02 uchebnogo goda, soglasno "Gosudarstvennoy programme razvitiya obrazovaniya", v Armenii deystvuet 11-letnyaya sistema obshchego srednego obrazovaniya. Vmeste s tem, razvitie estestvennonauchnoy mysli Armenii voskhodit svoimi kornyami k drevneyshim periodam chelovecheskoy tsivilizatsii. Lunnyy, solnechnyy kalendari primenyalis v povsednevnoy zhizni predkov armyanskogo naroda (pervyy vek do nashey ery - pyatyy vek do nashey ery). Ego predstavitelyami byli mnogie zamechatelnye uchenye drevnosti - astronomy, vrachi, zodchie, filosofy i matematiki. Tem ne menee, sistemnye nauchnye issledovaniya stali vozmozhny na territorii Armenii tolko v sovetskiy period vremeni. Dolgaya doroga Armenii k liderstvu v IT. Segodnya situatsiya s nauchnymi issledovaniyami skhozha s polozheniem nauki v drugikh stranakh SNG: iznoshennost materialnoy bazy, neobkhodimost investirovaniya v etu otrasl novykh sredstv i sokhraneniya nauchnogo potentsiala. Tem ne menee, blagodarya pomoshchi zarubezhnykh organizatsiy v Armenii provodyatsya vazhnye issledovaniya i nalazhivaetsya sotrudnichestvo s zarubezhnymi institutami. V internete deystvuet resurs Obshchestva Armyanskikh Arkhitektorov i Uchenykh Ameriki Armenian Engineers and Scientists of America (AESA). Podderzhivaetsya i institut entsiklopedii strany "Armenian Encyclopaedia Editorial Office" (Glavnaya Redaktsiya Armyanskoy Entsiklopedii). SMI Respubliki Armeniya. Uzhe v 1512 godu v Venetsii vyshli pervye pechatnye knigi na armyanskom yazyke. Pervaya armyanskaya tipografiya byla osnovana v Konstantinopole v 1567 godu, a v 1794 v Madrase (Indiya) vyshla pervaya armyanskaya ezhenedelnaya gazeta "Azdarar" ("Vestnik").

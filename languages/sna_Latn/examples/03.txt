Paanoisa makwai ake mumaoko evafudzi vari pasi pake, (vaya vanotungamirira) anonyatsotarisa kuti vari kuabata sei. Saka chimbofunga kuti Jehovha akanzwa sei ‘nezvevafudzi veIsraeri’ vakararama panguva yaEzekieri. Vatungamiriri ivavo vakanga vasinganyari kutonga vanhu ‘neutsinye vachivarwadzisa.’ Izvi zvakaita kuti makwai atambure, akawanda acho achitosiya kunamata kwakachena.—Ezek

# Source: Wikipedia article "Mukosho" (https://sn.wikipedia.org/)

Mukosho inengo iri seri kunobuda nako dhodhi. Amwe mazita anoshandiswa anosanganidzira rekuti horo, mhata. 
Mukosho ndoburi rekupedzisira iro rinotangira kumuromo richibatanidzwa mukati nematumbu. Basa rare rikuru nderekuburitsa zvekudya zvinenge zvatorwa zvinosimbisa muviri mukati memudumbu. 
Kune vamwe vanhu vanoshandisa nhengo iyi kuwana mufadzo. Vanoshandisa kumashure uku senzvimbo yesikarudzi, vana baba vachiisa nhengo yavo imomo kuti vanakirwe.

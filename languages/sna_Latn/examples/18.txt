11 dzichangochaya vapanduki vakabva vavhara migwagwa kuti motokari dzisafamba nechekukuchamhembe kweWhitehall. Nguva dzakanga dzava 11:20 mapurisa paakaita kuti vanhu vairatidzira vafambire kunze kwemugwagwa, sezvo motokari dzakanga dzava kuwanda. Nenguva dza11:29, vanhu vairatidzira vakakwidza kuWhitehall, vachibva vazopfuura kuTrafalgar Square, iri pedyo neStrand, ndokupfuura Aldwych vachikwidza nekuKingsway vakananga kuHolborn, uko kwakanga kuine bato reConservative Party iro rakanga richiitira musangano weSpring Forum muhotera inonzi Grand Connaught Rooms.

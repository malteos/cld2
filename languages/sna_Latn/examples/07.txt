# Source: Wikipedia article "Mapisarema" (https://sn.wikipedia.org/)

N'wadhi ra Mapisarema (Chihebheru: תְּהִלִּים, Tehillim, kureva. "Zvirumbidzo") kana Nduyo muChindau, rinozivikanwa wo sokuti Mupisarita, in'wadhi rekutanga mu-Ketuvim ("Zvinyorwa"), muchikamu chitatu muTanakh, uye in'wadhi remuTestamende Yekare. Dumidzo rayo rakabva kushanduro ye Chigiriki, ψαλμοί (psalmoi), kureva "mhanzi yechiridzwa" uye, sepakurebesa, "mashoko anofambinzana nemhanzi".

Akaberekerwa muHonh Kong, Ma akadzidza paYunivhesiti yeNew York uye Harvad Law School, uye akatombova mugari weAmerica wechigarire "wekadhi regirini". Hsieh aireva paiitwa sarudzo kuti Ma aikwanisa kutiza munyika panguva yekunetseka nekusagadzikana. Hsieh akaitawonharo kuti uyo Ma aibuda zvakanakisa pamifananidzo aingoratidzika zvakanaka asi pasina nezvemunhu kana udzamu. Pasinei nezvese zvaari kupomerwa,Ma akakunda uku aachitotsigira kuva neushamwari neChina. Mutambi anga achigona chaizvo nhasi anonzi Alex Ovechkin, uyo anotambira chikwata cheWashington Capitals.

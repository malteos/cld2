# Occitan grammar

- **Word order**: SVO; topicalisation allowed. Clitic pronouns proclitic to finite verbs, enclitic to infinitives, imperatives, and gerunds (*dona-me-lo, dire-te-o*).
- **Morphology**: Fusional. Conservative among Romance — retains two-term proximal/distal demonstratives plus a three-term system in some dialects.
- **Nouns**: Two genders (M/F); plural in `-s` (often not pronounced). Definite articles Languedocien `lo la los las`, Gascon `eth era eths eras` (unique), Provençal `lo/li` variants; indefinite `un una uns unas`.
- **Adjectives**: Agree in gender/number; typically postnominal.
- **Verbs**: Three conjugations (`-ar -er/-re -ir`). Rich tense paradigm: present, imperfect, preterite (still active), future, conditional, present/imperfect subjunctive, imperative. Compound tenses with auxiliary `aver` (generally) and occasionally `èsser`. Gascon features a unique "enunciative" particle `que / e / ja` preceding finite verbs in affirmative clauses (*que parli*).
- **Pronouns**: Pro-drop (Languedocien) or partially marked by enunciatives (Gascon). Weak clitics `me te se nos vos lo la los las li lor ne i` (cognates of Catalan / Italian *ne, y*).
- **Phonology**: Seven- or eight-vowel system with open/close contrast on `e o`; `o` written represents /u/ (like Portuguese/Catalan in some dialects), while `ò` = /ɔ/. Final consonants often silent. Palatals `nh lh`; Gascon has `sh` (/ʃ/) and intervocalic `-n-` loss (*lua* vs. *luna*).
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; preverbal negation `non` and/or postverbal `pas` (cognates of French *ne…pas*). Gascon *enunciative* particles are a diagnostic innovation absent in French and Catalan.

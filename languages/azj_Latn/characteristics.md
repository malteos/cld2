# North Azerbaijani characteristics

## Script signals

Latin alphabet with `ə ç ğ ı i ö ş ü` plus phonemic `q` and `x`.
**`ə` (schwa, U+0259)** is by far the strongest diagnostic against
Turkish; a single `ə` in a Latin-script Turkic sentence is nearly
conclusive evidence for Azerbaijani. Native `q` word-initial
(*qardaş, qız, qapı, qara*) and `x` (*xoş, çox, xalq*) reinforce
the signal. The case-contrasting i/ı pair mirrors Turkish.

## Top function words

*və, bir, bu, o, üçün, ilə, də, da, ki, amma, lakin, və ya, çox,
daha, ən, var, yox, deyil, mən, sən, biz, siz, onlar, kimi, qədər,
sonra, əvvəl, artıq, həm, isə, mı, nə, necə, niyə, harada*.

## N-gram and spelling patterns

- Locative `-də/-da`, ablative `-dən/-dan`, dative `-ə/-a`.
- Plural `-lər/-lar`.
- Present `-ir/-ür/-ır/-ur` + person.
- Future `-əcək/-acaq`.
- Copula `-dir/-dır/-dur/-dür`; enclitic past *idi* / fused
  *-di*.
- Frequent schwa n-grams: `ər`, `əl`, `əm`, `ən`, `ədə`, `əsi`,
  `mən`, `səhər`, `gələn`.
- `qa`, `qı`, `qo`, `qu` and `xa`, `xo`, `xü`, `xə` are common
  openings.

## Morphology hooks

- Dative *-ə* on front-harmony stems is a very high-precision cue.
- Present tense *-ir* often surfaces as `-ir` regardless of rounding
  in colloquial writing, competing with *-ür/-ur*.
- Verbal negation *-ma/-mə* attaches before tense.
- Interrogative clitic *mı/mi/mu/mü* is a separate orthographic
  word.

## Punctuation and orthography

Standard Latin punctuation. Quotations use `“…”` or `«…»`. Comma
is the decimal separator. Proper-noun + suffix boundaries are
sometimes marked by an apostrophe (like Turkish) though less
consistently.

# North Azerbaijani grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV, with modifiers before their heads.
  Word order is relatively flexible because case suffixes carry
  grammatical role.
- **Morphology**: Strongly agglutinative with transparent suffix
  boundaries. Vowel harmony across nine vowels
  (`a e ə i ı o ö u ü`) governs allomorphy; `ə` patterns as a
  front unrounded low vowel.
- **Nouns**: No grammatical gender, no articles. Six cases:
  nominative (∅), accusative *-(n)ı/-(n)i/-(n)u/-(n)ü*, dative
  *-a/-ə*, locative *-da/-də*, ablative *-dan/-dən*, genitive
  *-ın/-in/-un/-ün*. Plural *-lar/-lər*. Possessive suffixes
  (*-ım/-im/-um/-üm* etc.) precede case suffixes.
- **Verbs**: Suffix chain in order: negation *-ma/-mə*, ability
  *-(y)a bil-*, tense/aspect (*-dı/-di/-du/-dü* past, *-mış/-miş*
  reported past, *-ır/-ir/-ur/-ür* present, *-acaq/-əcək* future,
  *-ar/-ər* aorist), personal ending. Copula clitic
  *-dır/-dir/-dur/-dür*; shortened bare *-di* occurs colloquially.
- **Phonology**: Nine-vowel system. Voiced/voiceless obstruent
  contrasts include /q/ (written `q`) vs /k/ and /x/ (written `x`)
  vs /h/. Stress is normally final. Unlike Turkish, word-final
  voiced obstruents are retained in spelling (*kitab*, not *kitap*).
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. No overt copula in present affirmative
  (*bu kitab yaxşıdır* "this book is good"). Negation is suffixal.
  Polar questions use the clitic *mı/mi/mu/mü*. Relative clauses
  use participial constructions (*-an/-ən*, *-dığı/-diyi*).

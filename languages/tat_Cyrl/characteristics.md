# Tatar characteristics

## Script signals

Cyrillic script with the **six Tatar extras**: `ә ө ү җ ң һ`.
Strongest Tatar-specific cue is **`җ`** (U+04C7 / lowercase U+04C8),
which marks /dʒ/ and does not occur in Bashkir or Russian. `ң` and
`һ` are shared with other Turkic Cyrillic alphabets (Bashkir,
Kazakh). `ә ө ү` are shared with Bashkir but frequent enough to
anchor Turkic-vs-Russian decisions. The `ё ъ ь ц щ` Russian letters
appear mainly in loanwords.

## Top function words

*һәм, да, дә, та, тә, бу, шул, ул, алар, бер, өчен, белән, яки, ләкин,
әмма, тик, тагын, бик, күп, бар, юк, түгел, мин, син, без, сез, алар,
кебек, кадәр, соң, элек, ничек, кайда, кайчан, нигә, ни, нәрсә, кем*.

## N-gram and spelling patterns

- Plural `-лар`, `-ләр`.
- Locative `-да/-дә/-та/-тә`; ablative `-дан/-дән/-тан/-тән`.
- Genitive `-ның/-нең`.
- Present `-ый/-и` + person and past `-ды/-де` + person.
- Common Tatar words and clitics: *белән* ("with"), *иде* (past
  copula), *кебек* ("like"), *әле* ("still").
- Frequent trigrams: `лар`, `ләр`, `ның`, `нең`, `быз`, `сез`,
  `гән`, `ган`, `дыр`, `дер`.

## Morphology hooks

- Dative `-га/-гә`, which also occurs in Bashkir but in Tatar
  behaves harmonically with front `ә` in stems that Bashkir would
  have differently spelt.
- Verbal participle `-ган/-гән` (perfective) is highly productive.
- Copula clitic *-дыр/-дер* is optional and infrequent versus
  Azerbaijani/Turkish.

## Punctuation and orthography

Standard Cyrillic punctuation; «…» for quotations, comma for
decimals. Capitalisation follows Russian norms. `ъ` and `ь` appear
almost exclusively in Russian loans.

# Tatar grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV with modifier–head ordering.
  Relatively free due to case marking.
- **Morphology**: Strongly agglutinative with long suffix chains.
  Vowel harmony along a back/front axis (and partial roundness)
  governs suffix allomorphs.
- **Nouns**: No grammatical gender. Six cases: nominative (∅),
  genitive *-ның/-нең*, dative/directional *-га/-гә/-ка/-кә*,
  accusative *-ны/-не*, locative *-да/-дә/-та/-тә*, ablative
  *-дан/-дән/-тан/-тән*. Plural *-лар/-ләр*. Possessive suffixes
  attach before case: *-ым/-ем, -ың/-ең, -ы/-е, -ыбыз/-ебез,
  -ыгыз/-егез, -ы/-е*.
- **Verbs**: Suffix order: negation *-ма/-мә*, ability *-а/-ә ал-*,
  tense (*-ды/-де* definite past, *-ган/-гән* perfective,
  *-а/-ә/-ый/-и* present, *-ачак/-әчәк* future, *-ыр/-ер* aorist),
  person. Copula is usually zero in present; past copula *иде*.
- **Phonology**: Nine-vowel system including `ә ө ү ы е`. Distinctive
  phonemes /dʒ/ written **`җ`**, /ŋ/ written **`ң`**, /h/ written
  **`һ`**. Stress is typically word-final. Vowel reduction is
  moderate; vowel harmony is relatively strict.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. Present tense has no overt copula
  (*ул укытучы* "he is a teacher"). Negation is suffixal for verbs
  and via *түгел* for nominal predicates. Polar questions use the
  clitic *-мы/-ме*. Participial relatives (*-ган/-ә торган*) build
  subordinate clauses.

# Bulgarian grammar

- **Word order**: Predominantly SVO; topicalisation and clitic placement drive
  variation. Bulgarian is **analytic** by Slavic standards — the Balkan
  Sprachbund shapes its syntax.
- **Morphology**: Largely analytic on the noun, synthetic on the verb.
  Unlike every other Slavic language, Bulgarian (and Macedonian) has **lost
  the productive nominal case system**; only vestigial vocative and some
  pronominal cases remain.
- **Nouns**: Three genders (masculine, feminine, neuter), two numbers
  (plus a "count form" for masculine nouns after numerals, `два стола`).
  A **post-posed definite article** is the most salient feature:
  - masculine `-ът/-ят` (full) and `-а/-я` (short subject-object distinction)
  - feminine `-та`
  - neuter `-то`
  - plural `-те` (or `-та` for neuters)
  Adjectives agree in gender/number and also take the article enclitically
  when they are the first element (`голямата къща` "the big house").
- **Verbs**: Extremely rich tense system — nine tenses by most counts:
  present, aorist, imperfect, perfect, pluperfect, future, future-in-the-past,
  future perfect, future perfect in the past. A distinctive **renarrative /
  evidential** mood distinguishes witnessed from reported action. **No
  infinitive** — replaced by `да` + finite verb (`искам да работя` "I want to
  work"). Person/number endings mark all finite forms.
- **Phonology**: 6 vowels /a e i o u ɤ/ (the central ɤ is spelled **ъ**).
  Reduction of unstressed vowels: /a/ → /ɐ/, /o/ → /u/, /e/ → /i/ in some
  dialects and in casual speech. Palatalisation contrast survives before
  back vowels (marked with `ь` before о: `шофьор`).
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop; clitic doubling of objects (`Книгата я прочетох` "The
  book, I read it"); second-position clitics (present copula `е`, dative/
  accusative pronouns `ми, ти, му, ѝ`, interrogative `ли`). Negation with
  preverbal `не`.

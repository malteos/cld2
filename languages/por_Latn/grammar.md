# Portuguese Grammar

## Typology

- **Word order:** SVO, with VS order in presentational sentences (*Chegou o comboio / o trem*).
- **Morphology:** fusional; rich verbal inflection; nouns inflect for gender and number only.
- **Pro-drop:** null subjects are standard in European Portuguese (PE); Brazilian Portuguese (PB) uses overt subject pronouns much more often (*Eu acho que ele vem*).
- **Alignment:** nominative-accusative.

## Phonology (brief)

- **Oral vowels:** seven stressed /a ɛ e i ɔ o u/; heavy unstressed reduction in PE (unstressed *e* often [ɨ] or deleted, so *telefone* sounds like "tlfon"), milder in PB.
- **Nasal vowels and diphthongs:** /ɐ̃ ẽ ĩ õ ũ/ and *ão, ãe, õe*, written with tilde or with a following *m/n* (*mão, mãe, põe, campo, bem*). Absent from Spanish, Catalan and standard Galician.
- **Palatals** written **nh** /ɲ/ and **lh** /ʎ/ (*vinho, filho*); **ch** /ʃ/ (*chave*), **x** /ʃ/ or /ks/ (*xícara, táxi*), **j/g(e,i)** /ʒ/ (*já, gente*).
- **Sibilants:** *s/ss/ç/c(e,i)* /s/, intervocalic *s* and *z* /z/. PB palatalizes /t d/ before [i] (*tia* [tʃia]); syllable-final *s* is [ʃ] in PE and Rio de Janeiro.
- Stress is mostly penultimate; written accents: acute (*é, á*), circumflex for closed vowels (*ê, ô, â*), grave only for crasis (*à = a + a*).

## Nouns, Articles, Adjectives

- **Gender:** masculine/feminine.
- **Plural:** *-s* (*casa → casas*); *-r/-z → -es* (*mulher → mulheres*); *-m → -ns* (*homem → homens*); *-l → -is* (*animal → animais*, *papel → papéis*); *-ão* has three plurals (*mãos, pães, nações*).
- **Articles:** definite *o, a, os, as*; indefinite *um, uma, uns, umas*.
- **Obligatory contractions** of prepositions *de, em, a, por* with articles and demonstratives:
  - *de + o = do, da, dos, das*; *em + o = no, na, nos, nas*; *em + um = num, numa*;
  - *a + o = ao, aos*; *a + a = à, às* (crasis); *por + o = pelo, pela, pelos, pelas*;
  - *de + este = deste*, *em + isso = nisso*, *de + ele = dele*, *a + aquele = àquele*.
- Adjectives agree and usually follow the noun (*uma casa branca*).

## Pronouns

- Subject: *eu, tu, você, ele, ela, nós, (a gente), vós (archaic/regional), vocês, eles, elas*. PB uses *você* as the ordinary "you" and *a gente* "we"; PE keeps *tu* in informal speech.
- Clitics: *me, te, se, o, a, lhe, nos, vos, os, as, lhes*; the 3rd-person object forms change after verbs: *-lo/-la* after *-r/-s/-z* (*fazê-lo*), *-no/-na* after nasals (*dão-no*).
- **Placement:** PE uses enclisis in affirmative main clauses with a **hyphen** (*Disse-me*, *Chama-se João*), proclisis after negation and many subordinators (*Não me disse*). **Mesoclisis** in future/conditional (*dir-lhe-ei* "I will tell him", *far-se-ia*), mostly formal. PB prefers proclisis everywhere (*Me disse*, *Se chama João*).

## Verbs

Three conjugations: **-ar, -er, -ir** (*falar, comer, partir*), plus irregular *pôr*.

| | falar, present | preterite | imperfect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1sg | falo | falei | falava |
| 2sg | falas | falaste | falavas |
| 3sg | fala | falou | falava |
| 1pl | falamos | falamos (PE *falámos*) | falávamos |
| 3pl | falam | falaram | falavam |

- Note **-ou** (3sg preterite) and **-am/-em** (3pl), versus Spanish *-ó, -an* and Galician *-ou, -an*.
- **Perfect tenses** with *ter* (*tenho falado* = "I have been speaking", iterative; *tinha falado* = pluperfect). The simple preterite covers the English present perfect.
- **Synthetic pluperfect** *falara* survives in written registers.
- **Personal (inflected) infinitive:** *para nós falarmos*, *é melhor eles saírem* — shared only with Galician among the major Romance languages.
- **Future subjunctive** is fully alive: *quando eu for*, *se você quiser*.
- **Ser vs estar**; existential **há** (PE, formal) or **tem** (PB colloquial: *tem muita gente aqui*).
- Progressive: PE **estar a + infinitive** (*estou a falar*), PB **estar + gerund** (*estou falando*).
- Future: synthetic *falarei* (formal), periphrastic **ir + infinitive** (*vou falar*) in speech.

## Syntax

- Negation: preverbal **não**; negative concord (*Não vi ninguém*); PB also *não... não* and final *não* (*Sei não*).
- Questions use *que/o que, quem, como, onde, quando, porquê/por que*; PE often adds *é que* (*Onde é que estás?*).
- Complementizer *que*; relative *que, o qual, cujo*.
- Possessives usually take the article in PE (*o meu carro*), optionally in PB (*meu carro*).

## Short Examples

- *Não sei se ela já chegou.* "I don't know if she has arrived yet." (*não*, *já*, preterite *-ou*)
- PE *Estou a trabalhar; liga-me amanhã.* / PB *Estou trabalhando; me liga amanhã.* "I'm working; call me tomorrow." (progressive and clitic placement contrast)
- *É importante nós sabermos a verdade.* "It is important that we know the truth." (personal infinitive)
- *Quando vocês vierem, trago o livro.* "When you come, I'll bring the book." (future subjunctive)

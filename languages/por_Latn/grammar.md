# Portuguese grammar

- **Word order**: SVO; interrogatives by intonation or word order change. Object clitics are **proclitic in Brazilian Portuguese** (*me disse, te vi*) but **enclitic or mesoclitic in European Portuguese** (*disse-me, dir-me-á*) — a strong PT-PT vs. PT-BR signal.
- **Morphology**: Fusional. Verbs heavily inflected; unusual among Romance in retaining an inflected personal infinitive (*para comermos, ao chegares*).
- **Nouns**: Two genders (M/F). Plurals in `-s / -es`; nasal singulars in `-ão` pluralise variably (`-ões, -ães, -ãos`: *coração→corações, pão→pães, mão→mãos*). Definite articles `o a os as`; indefinite `um uma uns umas`. Preposition+article contractions: `do/da/dos/das, no/na, pelo/pela, ao/à, num/numa`.
- **Adjectives**: Agree in gender and number; typically postnominal; a closed class precedes.
- **Verbs**: Three conjugations (`-ar -er -ir` plus irregular `pôr`). Retains the preterite/perfect distinction; future subjunctive (*se fores, quando chegares*) is fully productive — a hallmark of Portuguese. Compound tenses use `ter` (preferred) or `haver` as auxiliary. Two copulas `ser`/`estar`.
- **Pronouns**: Pro-drop. Distinctive PT-PT clitic placement with mesoclisis in future/conditional (*far-te-ei, dir-te-ia*). Polite `o senhor / a senhora / você (BR)` vs. `tu (PT)`.
- **Phonology**: Seven-vowel oral + five nasal vowels. Distinctive nasal diphthongs `ão ãe õe`. PT-PT has vowel reduction (unstressed `e → ɨ`, `o → u`); PT-BR preserves fuller vowels.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop, preverbal negation `não`. Personal infinitive and future subjunctive are rare Romance features.

# Bashkir grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV. Modifier–head ordering
  (adjective–noun, genitive–noun). Flexibility is high because of
  case marking.
- **Morphology**: Strongly agglutinative. Vowel harmony along
  front/back and partial roundness axes; consonant harmony also
  affects suffix-initial obstruents (`-да/-дә/-та/-тә`).
- **Nouns**: No grammatical gender. Six cases: nominative (∅),
  genitive *-ҙың/-ҙең/-тың/-тең/-дың/-дең/-ның/-нең*,
  dative *-ға/-гә/-ҡа/-кә*, accusative *-ҙы/-ҙе/-ты/-те/-ны/-не*,
  locative *-да/-дә/-та/-тә*, ablative *-нан/-нән/-дан/-дән/-тан/-тән*.
  Plural *-лар/-ләр*. Possessives attach before case.
- **Verbs**: Suffix chain: negation *-ма/-мә*, ability *-а/-ә ал-*,
  tense (*-ны/-не* definite past, *-ған/-гән/-ҡан/-кән* perfective,
  *-а/-ә/-й* present, *-асаҡ/-әсәк/-ясаҡ/-йәсәк* future), person.
  Copula clitic *-дыр/-дер*.
- **Phonology**: Nine vowels including front `ә ө ү`. Distinctive
  consonants include /θ/ **`ҫ`**, /ð/ **`ҙ`**, /q/ **`ҡ`**, /ɣ/
  **`ғ`**, /ŋ/ `ң`, /h/ `һ`. Word-level stress is usually final.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. Zero copula in present affirmative; past
  copula *ине*. Nominal negation via *түгел*. Polar questions via
  clitic *-мы/-ме*. Participial subordination replaces finite
  relative clauses.

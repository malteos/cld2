# Bashkir characteristics

## Script signals

Cyrillic script with **nine Turkic extras**: `ә ө ү ң һ ҙ ҫ ҡ ғ`.
The strongest Bashkir-specific cues are the **interdental letters
`ҙ` (U+04A9) and `ҫ` (U+04AB)** — these are near-unique worldwide.
Also very diagnostic are uvular **`ҡ` (U+04A1)** and fricative
**`ғ` (U+0493)**. `ә ө ү ң һ` are shared with Tatar and other
Turkic Cyrillic languages, but the combination of those with
`ҙ/ҫ/ҡ/ғ` uniquely identifies Bashkir.

## Top function words

*һәм, менән, да, дә, та, тә, ул, был, шул, бер, өсөн, йәки, ләкин,
әммә, тик, тағы, бик, күп, бар, юҡ, түгел, мин, һин, беҙ, һеҙ, улар,
кеүек, тиклем, һуң, элек, нисек, ҡайҙа, ҡасан, нимә, кем*.

## N-gram and spelling patterns

- Plural `-лар/-ләр`.
- Locative `-да/-дә/-та/-тә`; ablative `-нан/-нән/-дан/-дән`.
- Genitive `-ҙың/-ҙең/-тың/-тең`.
- Perfective `-ған/-гән/-ҡан/-кән`.
- Frequent Bashkir bigrams/trigrams: `ҙы`, `ҙе`, `ҫа`, `ҫә`, `ҡа`,
  `ҡы`, `ғы`, `ғо`, `ҙың`, `ҫын`, `ҡан`, `ғән`.
- The surface pattern "vowel + `ҙ` + vowel" (intervocalic `ҙ`) is
  common (*күҙ, һүҙ, быҙау*).

## Morphology hooks

- Where Tatar has `з/с`, Bashkir systematically has `ҙ/ҫ`
  (*Tatar* **с**у ↔ *Bashkir* **һ**ыу "water"; *Tatar* кыҙ ↔
  *Bashkir* **ҡ**ыҙ "girl"; *Tatar* **с**үз ↔ *Bashkir* **һ**үҙ
  "word").
- Word-initial `һ-` where related languages have `с-` is another
  Bashkir-only pattern (*һыу, һүҙ, һин*).

## Punctuation and orthography

Standard Cyrillic punctuation; «…» for quotation. Comma decimal.
Capitalisation follows Russian conventions. Russian loanwords keep
Russian spelling and thus can reintroduce `ц щ ё ъ ь`.

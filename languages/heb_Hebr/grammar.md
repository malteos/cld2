# Modern Hebrew grammar

- **Word order**: **SVO** dominant in main clauses (shift from Biblical VSO).
  Fusional Semitic morphology.
- **Morphology**: Root-and-pattern (שורש + משקל / בנין). Verbal stems (binyanim)
  are seven: קל/פעל, נפעל, פיעל, פועל, הפעיל, הופעל, התפעל, each associated with
  voice/valency. Noun patterns (mishkalim): מקום, מפעל, קטלון, קטילה, etc.
  Strong use of clitics: definite article **הַ-** *ha-*, prepositions **בְּ-**,
  **לְ-**, **כְּ-**, **מִ-**, conjunction **וְ-**, object marker **אֶת**.
- **Nouns**: Two genders (masc/fem). Two numbers (sing/pl); dual survives only
  for time, body parts, and some pairs (ידיים, עיניים, שנתיים). No case;
  definiteness marked by **ה** prefix. Construct state (smikhut) expresses
  possession: *beit sefer* "book-of school" = "school".
- **Verbs**: Three tenses (past עבר, present הווה — also functions as active
  participle and gender-agreeing, future עתיד) plus imperative. Person/number/
  gender agreement via prefixes and suffixes (אני כותב / אני כותבת, אכתוב /
  תכתבי / יכתבו).
- **Phonology**: Simpler than Biblical — collapsed pharyngeals (ח ע often
  velar/glottal in Ashkenazi-influenced speech), /r/ typically uvular [ʁ].
  Five vowels (a, e, i, o, u). Stress usually final or penultimate.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop in past and future (agreement encodes subject);
  present tense requires overt subject since the form is participial. Present
  "be" copula absent; **יש / אין** express existential "there is/isn't".
  Accusative definite direct objects marked by **את** (*et*): אני אוהב את
  הכלב "I love the dog". Question particle **?** only; WH-in-situ possible
  but fronting common (מה, מי, איפה, מתי, למה, איך).

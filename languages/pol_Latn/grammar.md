# Polish grammar

- **Word order**: Default SVO, but free in practice because case morphology
  marks grammatical roles; word order serves information structure. Fusional
  and strongly synthetic.
- **Morphology**: Fusional with heavy suffixation. Productive derivational
  suffixes include `-ość` (abstract nouns), `-anie/-enie` (nominalisations),
  `-ski/-cki` (relational adjectives), `-ować` (verbalisers).
- **Nouns**: Three genders in the singular (masculine, feminine, neuter) and
  a two-way split in the plural between **masculine-personal** (mężczyźni)
  and everything else (non-masculine-personal, including all feminine, neuter
  and non-human masculine). Seven cases: nominative, genitive, dative,
  accusative, instrumental, locative, **vocative** (productive, e.g.
  `panie → Panie!`). Adjective endings `-y/-i` (m), `-a` (f), `-e` (n/pl),
  `-ego, -emu, -ym, -ych, -ymi` in oblique cases.
- **Verbs**: Imperfective/perfective aspect pairs (`pisać`/`napisać`).
  Non-past endings `-ę, -esz, -e, -emy, -ecie, -ą` / `-ę, -isz, -i, -imy,
  -icie, -ą`. Past tense constructed from an `-ł-/-ła-/-ło-/-li-/-ły-`
  participle plus enclitic person endings `-m, -ś, -śmy, -ście` that are
  mobile (`pisałem` or `jam pisał`). Conditional with `-by-` + person
  endings. Imperative in `-j/-uj` or bare stem.
- **Phonology**: Nasal vowels /ɛ̃/ (ę) and /ɔ̃/ (ą) — a defining Lechitic
  feature. Rich sibilant inventory: three series /s z/, /ɕ ʑ/ (ś ź), /ʂ ʐ/
  (sz ż/rz). Affricates /ts dz/ (c, dz), /tɕ dʑ/ (ć dź), /tʂ dʐ/ (cz dż).
  The letter ł = /w/. Stress is fixed on the penultimate syllable.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop; no articles. Copula `być` present in the present
  tense (`jestem`, `jesteś`, `jest`, `jesteśmy`, `jesteście`, `są`).
  Negation preverbal `nie`; genitive of negation with objects of transitive
  verbs under negation.

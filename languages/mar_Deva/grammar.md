# Marathi grammar

- **Word order**: SOV; postpositional; modifiers precede the head.
- **Morphology**: Fusional with significant agglutinative traits. Three grammatical genders (masculine, feminine, neuter) — a key contrast with Hindi's two-gender system. Neuter is productive in Marathi (मूल "child", पुस्तक "book") and triggers distinct verb agreement.
- **Nouns**: Two numbers, seven cases (nominative, accusative, instrumental, dative, ablative, genitive, locative) marked by postposition-like suffixes **-ला, -ने, -हून, -चा/-ची/-चे, -त, -वर**. Oblique stems precede postpositions.
- **Genitive**: **-चा** (m.sg), **-ची** (f.sg), **-चे** (m.pl / n.), **-च्या** (f.pl / obl.) — highly productive and a Marathi fingerprint. Hindi equivalent is **-का / -की / -के**.
- **Pronouns**: मी (I), तू (you), तो / ती / ते (he / she / it), आम्ही / आपण (we, exclusive / inclusive!), तुम्ही (you-pl/polite), ते (they). Inclusive/exclusive "we" (आपण vs आम्ही) is a rare Indo-Aryan feature.
- **Verbs**: Rich agreement in tense, person, number *and* gender (3 ways). Copula **आहे** (is), **आहेत** (are), past **होता / होती / होते**, future **-ईल / -ईन / -तील**, perfective **-ला / -ली / -ले**, habitual **-तो / -ते / -ती**. Ergative-style **ने** marking appears in perfective transitives, similar to Hindi.
- **Phonology**: Three retroflex consonants **ट ठ ड ढ ण** plus the retroflex lateral **ळ** /ɭ/ and occasional **ऴ**; three sibilants **स श ष**. Schwa at word-final is not normally deleted (contrast Hindi *राम* [ram] vs Marathi *राम* [raːm]).
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop; copula agreement with gender often suffices to recover the subject. Negation **नाही / नाहीत** (post-verbal) and **न / नका** (prohibitive). Relative-correlative **जो … तो** / **जे … ते**.

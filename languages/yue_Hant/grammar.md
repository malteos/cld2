# Cantonese grammar

- **Word order**: SVO with topic-comment patterning, similar to Mandarin. Modifiers precede heads; adverbials precede the verb.
- **Morphology**: Isolating/analytic. Aspect and modality marked by post-verbal particles and verbal suffixes. No inflection.
- **Nouns**: No gender, no case, no obligatory number marking. 啲 marks indefinite plural/some: 啲人 (people / some people). Classifiers are obligatory and can stand alone as definite markers: 本書 = "the book". Possession with 嘅: 我嘅書 (my book).
- **Verbs**: Aspect particles 咗 (perfective, = Mandarin 了), 過 (experiential), 緊 (progressive, = Mandarin 着/在), 住 (durative), 晒 (completive/totalitive), 返 (back/again). Modal verbs 會 可以 應該 要 precede the verb. Copula is 係 (not 是).
- **Phonology**: Six tones (Hong Kong system; some analyses count 9 by splitting checked tones). Preserves final stops -p, -t, -k and final -m, unlike Mandarin. Syllable inventory is more complex than Mandarin.
- **Syntax notes**: Heavy use of sentence-final particles 啦 喇 咩 呀 喎 囉 嘅 吖 嘛 嚟 — clustering multiple particles in one utterance is grammatical. Negation: 唔 (atelic) vs 冇 / 未 (perfective/existential). Yes/no questions use final 嗎/咩 or A-not-A: 去唔去. Comparative uses 過: 佢高過我 ('he is taller than me'). 畀 serves as 'give' and as passive marker: 佢畀人打咗.

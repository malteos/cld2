# Amharic LID characteristics

## Script signals

**Ethiopic (Ge'ez) script** U+1200–U+137F — the script alone narrows LID to
Ethio-Semitic (amh, tir, gez, tig, etc.). Amharic-diagnostic letters include:
- **ሸ ሹ ሺ ሻ ሼ ሽ ሾ** (š series, U+1238–U+123F) — common in amh, rarer in gez.
- **ቸ ቹ ቺ ቻ ቼ ች ቾ** (č series, U+1278–U+127F).
- **ኘ ኙ ኚ ኛ ኜ ኝ ኞ** (ñ, palatal nasal series, U+1298–U+129F) — Amharic hallmark
  (not in Ge'ez).
- **ዠ ዡ ዢ ዣ ዤ ዥ ዦ** (ž series, U+12E0–U+12E7).
- **ጀ ጁ ጂ ጃ ጄ ጅ ጆ** (ǧ/j series, U+1300–U+1307) — frequent in amh, rare in gez.
- Ejective **ጠ ጩ ጵ ቅ ጸ** signal Ethio-Semitic but amh mixes ጠ/ጩ frequently.

## Top function words

ነው (*näw*, is — masc), ናት (*nat*, is — fem), ናቸው (*načäw*, are), እና (*ənna*,
and), ወይም (*wäyəmm*, or), ግን (*gən*, but), ስለ (*səlä*, about/because), እንደ
(*əndä*, like/as/that), ከ- (from), ለ- (for/to), በ- (in/at/with), ወደ (*wädä*,
to/towards), ላይ (*lay*, on), ውስጥ (*wəsṭ*, inside), ጋር (*gar*, with), ሁሉ
(*hullu*, all), ብዙ (*bəzu*, many), ትንሽ (*tənnəš*, a little), ነገር (*nägär*,
thing/matter), ሰው (*säw*, person), ሀገር / አገር (*agär*, country), ቤት (*bet*,
house), ጊዜ (*gize*, time), ቀን (*qän*, day), ይህ / ይኸ (*yəh / yəhə*, this),
ያ / ያችን (*ya*, that), እኔ (*əne*, I), አንተ / አንቺ (*antä/antʃi*, you m/f), እሱ /
እሷ (*əssu/əsswa*, he/she), እኛ (*əñña*, we), እናንተ (*ənnantä*, you pl), እነሱ
(*ənnässu*, they), ሆነ (*honä*, became), ሄደ (*hedä*, went), አለ (*alä*, there
is / said).

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Verb suffix **-ም** (-m) at word end combined with clause-medial position is
  a hallmark of negation (አል-...-ም) and conjunction.
- Verb suffix clusters **-ኃል / -ኃለች / -ኃሉ** (future/present perfect auxiliary
  fusions): ይሄዳል *yəhedal* "he goes", ትሄዳለች *təhedalläčč* "she goes".
- Genitive prefix **የ-** (*yä-*) attached to preceding-noun modifier: የአዲስ አበባ
  "of Addis Ababa".
- Instrumental/locative prefix **በ-**, allative **ወደ**, ablative **ከ-**,
  dative/benefactive **ለ-** all written attached.
- Word **ነው** / **ነበር** / **ናቸው** as clause-final copula is the strongest
  surface Amharic signature.

## Morphology hooks

Negative circumfix **አል-...-ም** on past, **አይ-...-ም** on non-past (አልመጣም
"he didn't come", አይመጣም "he doesn't/won't come"). Definite suffix **-ው /
-ዋ** on nouns. Accusative **-ን**. Converb (gerund) forms in **-ቶ / -ተች / -ተው**
drive narrative chaining (መጥቶ ሄደ "having come, he went"). Cleft/relative
marker **የ-** prefixed to verb.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Ethiopic punctuation: **፡** (word separator, U+1361, historical), **።**
(full stop, U+1362), **፣** (comma, U+1363), **፤** (semicolon, U+1364),
**፥** (colon, U+1365), **፦** (preface colon, U+1366), **፧** (question mark,
U+1367), **፨** (paragraph separator). Amharic numerals U+1369–U+137C (፩ ፪ ፫
፬ ፭ ፮ ፯ ፰ ፱ ፲ ፳ ፴ ፵ ፶ ፷ ፸ ፹ ፺ ፻ ፼) occasionally appear in traditional
contexts; Western 0–9 dominate modern text.

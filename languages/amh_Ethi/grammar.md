# Amharic grammar

- **Word order**: **SOV** (head-final) — a major typological divergence from
  other Semitic languages. Verbs, postpositions, and genitive-before-head.
- **Morphology**: Root-and-pattern templatic morphology (Semitic inheritance)
  combined with **strongly agglutinating suffixation** of person/tense/object
  markers on the verb. Pronominal object and applicative suffixes stack
  (e.g. sät·t·ä·ññ·at "he gave it to me for her").
- **Nouns**: Two genders (masc/fem), but gender is overtly marked only for
  animates. Definite marker **-u** (masc) / **-wa / -itu** (fem) suffixed to
  noun. Plural **-očč** (ልጆች *ləǧočč* "children"). Accusative marker **-n**
  on definite direct objects: ልጁን አየሁ *ləǧun ayyähu* "I saw the boy".
- **Verbs**: Two basic stems — **perfective** (gerundive/past) and
  **imperfective** (non-past). Composite tenses built with auxiliary
  **allä / näbbär** (be-present / was). Future = imperfective + copula
  clitic. Negation is **circumfixal**: **አ-...-ም** (a-...-m) on the verb
  — አልሄደም *al-hed-ä-m* "he did not go".
- **Phonology**: Rich consonant inventory including **ejectives** ጥ ጭ ጵ ቅ
  ጽ (t', č', p', k', s'), palatals, and geminates (length phonemic). Seven
  vowels (ä, u, i, a, e, ə, o). Stress not phonemic.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; subject and object indexed on verb. Copula
  **ነው** *näw* (is, masc), **ናት** *nat* (is, fem), **ናቸው** *načäw* (are).
  Genitive is noun + **የ-** *yä-* + head: የልጁ መጽሐፍ *yä-ləǧu mäṣhaf* "the
  child's book" (modifier first). Subordinate clauses use converb (gerund)
  forms in -o / -e for narrative chaining.

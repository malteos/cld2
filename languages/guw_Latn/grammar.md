# Gun / Gungbe grammar

- **Word order**: **SVO**, head-initial. The nominal modifier (adjective, numeral, demonstrative) follows the noun: *àsɔ́ dàhó* "big crab".
- **Morphology**: Strongly **isolating**, like other Gbe languages. Grammatical relations are carried by free particles and tone, not inflection. Some productive reduplication for intensification and nominalisation.
- **Nouns**: No gender, no case, no inflected number. Plurality is marked by postposed **lɛ́** (*ɖé lɛ́* "some", *ɔ́ lɛ́* "the ones"). Definite **ɔ́** and indefinite **ɖé** follow the noun. Genitive is expressed by juxtaposition or by **tɔ̀n**: *xwé Kòfí tɔ̀n* "Kofi's house".
- **Verbs**: Uninflected stems with **preverbal TMA particles**: **tò / ɖò** (progressive, "be at"), **ná** (future/irrealis), **kò** (anterior/"already"), **má** / **mà** (negation). Serial verb constructions are pervasive: *é yì hɔ̀n á* "he went and bought it". There is no subject agreement; person is carried by independent pronouns (**un/nyɛ, a, é, mí, mìn, yé**).
- **Phonology**: Seven or eight oral vowels **i e ɛ a ɔ o u** plus nasalised counterparts (written *-n*). Three register tones (H, M, L), sometimes contour. Labial-velars **gb, kp**, labialised **hw, xw, kw, gw**, and the implosive **ɖ**. Vowel harmony (ATR) in some subdialects.
- **Syntax notes**: Copulas split by function: **nyí** (identity), **tò/ɖò** (location, existence), **gán** (become). Question marker **à?** at clause end. Clause-final particles mark emphasis (**lóó, lóo**) and topic (**ɔ́**).

# Crimean Tatar grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV, modifier–head ordering. Case
  marking allows order variation.
- **Morphology**: Strongly agglutinative. Vowel harmony along
  front/back and (partial) roundness axes. Consonant alternations
  include final obstruent devoicing and k/q ↔ g/ğ alternations
  before vowel-initial suffixes.
- **Nouns**: No gender, no article. Six cases: nominative (∅),
  genitive *-nıñ/-niñ/-nuñ/-nüñ*, dative *-ğa/-ge/-qa/-ke*,
  accusative *-nı/-ni/-nu/-nü*, locative *-da/-de/-ta/-te*,
  ablative *-dan/-den/-tan/-ten*. Plural *-lar/-ler*. Possessive
  suffixes precede case.
- **Verbs**: Suffix order: negation *-ma/-me*, ability *-a/-e bil-*,
  tense (*-dı/-di/-du/-dü* past, *-gan/-gen/-qan/-ken* perfective,
  *-a/-e* present, *-acaq/-ecek* future, *-ır/-ir/-ur/-ür* aorist),
  person. Copula *-dır/-dir/-dur/-dür*.
- **Phonology**: Eight vowels (a e ı i o ö u ü) and phonemic /q/
  (written `q`), /ɣ/ (written `ğ`), /x/ (written `x`), /ŋ/
  (written `ñ`). Stress is typically word-final.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. Null copula in present affirmative. Polar
  questions with clitic *-mı/-mi/-mu/-mü*. Participial relatives
  with *-gan/-ğan* and *-acaq/-ecek* replace finite relative
  clauses.

# Source: https://www.jw.org/st/ (jw.org Southern Sotho edition)

Re tsoa merabeng le lipuong tse sa tšoaneng, leha ho le joalo, re kopane ka morero o le mong. Ntho ea bohlokoa ka ho fetisisa ho rōna ke ho hlompha Jehova , e leng Mongoli oa Bibele le ’Mōpi oa lintho tsohle. Re etsa sohle seo re ka se khonang hore re etsise Jesu Kreste , ’me re motlotlo hore ebe re Bakreste . Hangata re qeta nako e itseng, re thusa batho ho ithuta Bibele le ka ’Muso oa Molimo . Kaha rea paka, kapa re bua ka Jehova Molimo le ’Muso oa hae, re bitsoa Lipaki Tsa Jehova .

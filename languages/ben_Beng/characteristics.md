# Bengali LID characteristics

## Script signals

- Bengali script block U+0980–U+09FF. Characteristic rounded letter shapes with an overhead bar.
- Left-placed dependent vowel sign **ি** (pre-base i-mātrā) is highly diagnostic — it visually precedes its consonant even though it is spoken after it.
- Anusvāra **ং** and visarga **ঃ** occur (visarga mostly in Sanskritic words).
- **Key differentiator from Assamese**: Bengali uses **র** for /r/ and **ব** where Assamese uses **ৰ** (r with hook) and **ৱ** (wavy ba). The absence of **ৰ / ৱ** is a Bengali-not-Assamese signal.
- Bengali-specific letters include **ৎ** (khanda ta) and **য়** (ya with nukta).

## Top function words

এবং, ও, কিন্তু, তবে, অথবা, বা, যে, যা, যিনি, যারা, যখন, যেখানে, এই, সেই, এটা, এটি, সেটা, হয়, হয়েছে, হবে, ছিল, আছে, নেই, না, হ্যাঁ, কি, কেন, কীভাবে, কোথায়, কখন, আমি, আমরা, তুমি, তোমরা, আপনি, সে, তারা, সবাই, কিছু, কেউ, অনেক.

## N-gram and spelling patterns

- Common word endings: **-এর** (genitive), **-কে** (accusative/dative), **-তে** (locative), **-রা** (animate plural), **-টা / -টি / -গুলো** (definite classifier).
- Very common verb tails **-ছে / -ছেন / -ছিল / -বে / -বেন** (progressive/perfect/past/future with honorific).
- Conjunction **এবং** and **ও** (both meaning "and") at word-initial or between clauses.
- Yes/no question particle **কি** at sentence end.

## Morphology hooks

- Classifier clitics **-টা / -টি** on nouns.
- Genitive marker **-এর** on the possessor noun.
- Perfective aspect in **-ছে / -ছেন** + subject-honorific suffix.
- Negation word **না** / **নি** placed after the verb.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

- Sentence terminator **।** (*daṛi*, shared with Devanagari).
- Digits are the Bengali set ০ ১ ২ ৩ ৪ ৫ ৬ ৭ ৮ ৯, but Latin digits are also frequent.
- Conjunct-heavy orthography; **্** (virāma/hasanta) appears between letters to suppress inherent /ɔ/.

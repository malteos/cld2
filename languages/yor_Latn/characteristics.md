# Yoruba LID characteristics

## Script signals

Yoruba text is saturated with **tone marks** and **underdot diacritics**, making it visually unmistakable:

- Under-dot letters **ẹ / Ẹ** (U+1EB9 / U+1EB8), **ọ / Ọ** (U+1ECD / U+1ECC), **ṣ / Ṣ** (U+1E63 / U+1E62).
- Tone marks on vowels: acute **◌́** (high) and grave **◌̀** (low). Combinations with underdots produce **ẹ́**, **ẹ̀**, **ọ́**, **ọ̀**. These precomposed/combining sequences are a near-certain Yoruba signal.
- No letters **c, q, v, x, z** in native vocabulary.
- Digraph **gb** appears frequently at word onset and is rarely found elsewhere (*gbogbo*, *gbà*, *ìgbà*, *agbára*).

## Top function words

**ni**, **kì**, **kò**, **kí**, **ti**, **ń**, **àti**, **pé**, **bí**, **sì**, **tí**, **àwọn**, **wọn**, **wa**, **yín**, **o**, **ó**, **mo**, **mi**, **èmi**, **ìwọ**, **òun**, **àwa**, **ẹ**, **ẹ̀yin**, **ló**, **ń ṣe**, **máa**, **yóò**, **ti ṣe**, **gbogbo**, **díẹ̀**, **kò sí**, **wà**, **jẹ́**, **fún**, **lórí**, **nínú**, **sí**, **pẹ̀lú**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Word-initial **gb-** (*gbogbo*, *gbà*, *gbé*, *gbọ́*) and **kp-**/**p-** labial-velars.
- Frequent final **-n** marking nasal vowels: *àwọn*, *ohun*, *ìran*, *gbogbo*.
- Reduplicated vowel CVCV stems (*bàtà, òwò, òpó, lọ́wọ́*).
- Prefix nominalisations **a-, o-, ì-, à-, ó-** at word start.
- Ubiquitous single-syllable particles with tones: *ní, sí, tí, bí, pé, kí, fún, mọ́*.

## Morphology hooks

- **Àwọn** before nouns = plural marker; extremely frequent.
- Preverbal TMA particles **ń, ti, máa, yóò, á, kò, kì í, má** cluster right before verbs.
- Nominalising reduplication *jó → jíjó*, *kà → kíkà*.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Some online sources drop tone marks entirely (leaving *oro* for both *ọrọ́* and *òròrò*) — a common low-resource variant. Under-dots may be approximated with trailing dots (`e.` `o.`) or vertical bars in legacy typewriter text. The endonym **Yorùbá** with the grave accent is itself a canonical LID fingerprint.

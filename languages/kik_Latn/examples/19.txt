Kĩndũ thaa ithano na ndagĩka mĩrongo ĩrĩ na kenda, maandamano macio magĩkinya Whitehall, kũhetũka Trafalgar Square, makabĩte Strand na kũhetũka Aldwych marorete Kingsway na merekeire Kingsway kũrĩa kĩama gĩa conservative kĩarĩ na mũchemanio wao wa Spring mũkawa inĩ wa Grand Connaught Rooms.

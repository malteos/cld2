Hsieh nĩtaonanirie atĩ hĩndĩ ya ithurano Ma no ore bũrũri ihinda ria ngahĩha icio. Hsieh noonanirie atĩ photogenic Ma nĩ mũndũ mũno wa style kũrĩ substance. Ona thengia wa maũndũ macio ma kũigĩrĩrwo, Ma nĩacindire nĩũndũ wakũonania ũnyitanĩri mũnene na Chinese Mainland. Mũthaki wa mũthenya wa ũmũthĩ nĩ Alex Ovechkin wa Washington Capitals. Arĩ na mbao igĩrĩ na agĩteithĩrĩria kũingĩria ingĩ igĩrĩ rĩrĩa team ya Washington yahotanire na mbao ithano gwa ithatũ magĩthaka na Atlanta Thrashers. Ovechkin ateithĩrĩirie team yake kũfunga mbao riita rĩa mbere ũtukũ ũcio hĩndĩ ĩrĩa mbao ya Rookie Nicklas Backstrom yamaheire ũhotani.

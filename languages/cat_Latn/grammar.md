# Catalan grammar

- **Word order**: SVO; fairly rigid. Questions by intonation or by word-order change. Clitic pronouns are proclitic to finite verbs, enclitic to infinitives/imperatives/gerunds, often bound with hyphen or apostrophe (*dóna'm, mira-la, veient-lo*).
- **Morphology**: Fusional. Rich clitic system; some pronouns have up to four surface forms depending on phonological context (*me / em / m' / 'm*).
- **Nouns**: Two genders (M/F). Plural `-s / -os` (after sibilants: *peixos, rojos*). Definite articles `el / la / els / les`, with `l'` before vowel-initial words; Balearic dialects use the "salat" article `es / sa / es / ses`. Indefinite `un una uns unes`.
- **Adjectives**: Agree in gender/number; usually postnominal.
- **Verbs**: Three conjugations (`-ar -er/-re -ir`). TAM includes present, imperfect, preterite (the periphrastic *vaig cantar* = "I sang" is unique among Romance — auxiliary *anar* + infinitive conveys simple past, not future), future, conditional, present/imperfect subjunctive, imperative. Compound tenses with `haver` + participle. Copulas `ser/ésser` and `estar`.
- **Pronouns**: Pro-drop. Weak object pronouns `em et es ens us el la els les li els hi ho en`. `hi` (locative) and `en` (partitive/genitive) are preserved from Latin — similar to French *y* and *en*.
- **Phonology**: Eight-vowel system with open/close `e o` contrast (like Italian); vowel reduction in unstressed syllables (*a e → ə*, *o u → u* in Central Catalan). Final devoicing of obstruents. Palatals `ny`, `ll`, `ix`.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop, preverbal negation `no`, productive use of `hi` and `en`, often doubled with full objects.

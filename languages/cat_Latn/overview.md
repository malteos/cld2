# Catalan (cat)

## Summary

Catalan (endonym: *català*) is a Romance language (Indo-European → Italic → Romance → Occitano-Romance), historically grouped with Occitan. ISO codes: 639-1 `ca`, 639-2 `cat`, 639-3 `cat`. Written in the Latin script. Around 9 million native speakers, ~10 million total. Official in Andorra (sole official), and co-official in the Spanish autonomous communities of Catalonia, Valencia (as *valencià*), and the Balearic Islands. Also spoken in the Roussillon region of southern France and in the city of Alghero in Sardinia. Approximately 4 million use it daily.

## Writing system

Uses the 26-letter Latin alphabet with diacritics: grave `à è ò`, acute `é í ó ú`, diaeresis `ï ü`, cedilla `ç`, and the **geminate-L** punt volat `l·l` (middle dot between two L's). The digraphs `ny` (equivalent to Spanish `ñ`), `l·l`, `ix`, `tx`, `tg/tj` are distinctive. Apostrophes occur for elision (`l'home, d'aigua, m'ha dit, s'ha fet`). Standard Institut d'Estudis Catalans orthography since 1913.

## Status

Co-official with Spanish (and French in Andorra). Actively regulated by the Institut d'Estudis Catalans (IEC) and the Acadèmia Valenciana de la Llengua (AVL). Catalan has a strong media and literary presence (TV3, Viquipèdia) and a large L2 population.

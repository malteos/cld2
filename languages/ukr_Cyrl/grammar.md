# Ukrainian grammar

- **Word order**: SVO by default but highly free owing to rich case marking.
  Information structure and prosody determine surface order. Fusional /
  synthetic.
- **Morphology**: Fusional with extensive suffixation and verbal prefixation.
  Aspect pairs built with prefixes (`писати` / `написати`) and stem
  alternation; the prefix inventory overlaps with Russian but `з-`, `роз-`
  and `ви-` are characteristically Ukrainian in distribution.
- **Nouns**: Three genders (masculine, feminine, neuter) and two numbers.
  Seven cases: nominative, genitive, dative, accusative, instrumental,
  locative, **vocative** (distinctive — the vocative is a fully productive
  case, e.g. `друг → друже`, `мама → мамо`, `Петро → Петре`). Adjective
  m.sg.nom ends in `-ий/-ій`, f. `-а/-я`, n. `-е/-є`, pl. `-і/-ї`.
- **Verbs**: Imperfective/perfective aspect. Tenses: past (with `-в/-ла/-ло/
  -ли`, gender-marked in sg.), present, future (synthetic `-му/-меш/-ме` or
  analytic `буду + inf`). Person/number endings `-ю/-у, -єш/-иш, -є/-ить,
  -ємо/-имо, -єте/-ите, -ють/-уть/-ать`. Conditional with particle `би/б`.
  Pluperfect survives marginally (`був зробив`).
- **Phonology**: 6 vowels /i ɪ e a o u/ (letter **и** = /ɪ/, **і** = /i/).
  Unlike Russian, there is **no akanye** — unstressed /o/ stays /o/.
  Consonants: /ɦ/ (letter г), /g/ (letter ґ), prominent /ts/ /dz/ affricates
  (spelled ц, дз), palatalised series. Alternations `г ~ з ~ ж`, `к ~ ц ~ ч`,
  `х ~ с ~ ш` in declension (e.g. `рука → руці → ручний`).
- **Syntax**: Optional pro-drop; present copula `є` is used but often omitted.
  No articles. Negation with preverbal `не`; `нема(є)` + GEN for existential
  negation. Reflexive/passive clitic `-ся` is written as part of the verb
  (`вмиватися`).

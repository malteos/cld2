A ranar Litinin, masana kimiyya daga Kwalejin Kimiyya ta Jami'ar Stanford suka sanar da ƙirƙirar sabon kayan aikin bincike wanda zai iya rarraba ƙwayoyin hallita ta nau'i: ƙaramin cip na bugu wanda za'a iya ƙerawa ta yin amfani da ingantacciyar firintar inkjet mai yiwuwar a kan kusan cent ɗaya na Amurka kowannensu. Manyan masu bincike sun ce wannan na iya sa a gano ciwon daji, tarin fuka, ƙanjamau da zazzaɓin cizon sauro da wuri ga marasa lafiya a ƙasashe masu ƙarancin kuɗaɗen shiga, inda adadin warkewa na cututtuka kamar sankarar nono zai iya kasancewa rabin na ƙasashe masu arziƙi.

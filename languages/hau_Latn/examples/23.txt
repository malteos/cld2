Ya kammala karatun sa daga Kwalejin Zane da kimiya na Jami’ar Virginia a 1950 kuma yana daga cikin manyan masu taimakawa cibiyar. An saka wa kurkukun Abu Ghraib na Iraq wuta lokacin hargitsi. Kurkukun ta yi ƙaurin suna bayan an gano cin zarafin fursunoni da ake yi a can bayan dakarun Amurka sun karɓi iko. Piquet Jr. ya faɗi a gasar Grand Prix ta 2008 a Singapore jim kaɗan bayan dakatawar wucin-gadi ta wuri da aka yi wa Fernando Alonso, inda aka fito da motar agaji. Yayin da motocin da ke gaban Alonso suka shiga don shan mai a mota mai ba da kariya, sai ya matsa sama don samun nasara.

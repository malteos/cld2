An haife shi a Hong Kong, Ma ya yi karatu a Jami'ar New York da Makarantar Koyon Aikin Shari’a ta Harvard kuma ya taɓa riƙe katin zama a Amurka na dindindin “Green card”. Hsieh ya yi nuni a lokacin zaben cewa Ma na iya tserewa daga ƙasar yayin rikici. Hsieh kuma ya bayar da hujjar cewa Ma mai kyau a hoto ya fi salo amman bashi da amfani. Duk da wadannan zarge-zargen, Ma yayi nasara da kyau a kan aiki a shafin da yake ta kiran a samu alaƙa ta kusa da cikin ƙasar China. Yau ɗan wasan da yafi nuna ƙwazo shi ne Alex Ovechkin na Washinton Capitals.

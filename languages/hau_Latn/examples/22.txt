Ya ci ƙwallaye 2 da kuma 2 da ya taimaka aka ci a nasarar wasan Washington 5-3 akan Atlanta Thrashers. Taimakon Ovechkin na farko a daren ta kasance sanadiyar lashe wasa ta rookie Nicklas Backstrom; ƙwallo na biyu da ya shigar a daren shi ne na 60 a kakar wasanni, haka nan ya zama ɗan wasa na farko da ya shigar da ƙwallaye 60 ko fiye da haka a cikin kakar wasanni tun daga 1995-96, a lokacin da Jaromir Jagr da Mario Lemieux kowanensu ya kai wancan matakin. An bai wa Batten matsayi na 190 a jerin sunayen Ƴan Amurka 400 mafi kuɗi a shekarar 2008 inda aka ƙiyasta dukiyarsa ta kai biliyan $2.3.

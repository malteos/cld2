# Hausa LID characteristics

## Script signals

Boko Hausa is the most reliable signal in sub-Saharan African Latin text: the **hooked letters ɓ ɗ ƙ** (U+0253, U+0257, U+0199 and capitals U+0181, U+018A, U+0198) are near-exclusive to Hausa and a handful of neighbouring Chadic/Fula orthographies. Optional **ƴ** (U+01B4) appears in some spellings of *ƴa*, *ƴan*. Apostrophe **'** marks glottal stop word-internally (*sha'awa*, *Allah'u*). Tone/length marks (macrons, grave) appear only in dictionaries.

## Top function words

**ne**, **ce**, **ba**, **na**, **da**, **a**, **ya**, **ta**, **sun**, **mun**, **kun**, **yana**, **tana**, **ina**, **muna**, **suna**, **kuma**, **wannan**, **wancan**, **don**, **domin**, **saboda**, **amma**, **ko**, **ko da**, **in**, **idan**, **wa**, **wani**, **wata**, **duk**, **dukan**, **ga**, **zuwa**, **a kan**, **cikin**, **kan**, **ma'ana**, **shi**, **ita**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Frequent initial sequences `gid-`, `yan-`, `shi-`, `mut-`, `ran-`, `mas-`, `tsa-`.
- Digraphs **sh**, **ts**, **fy**, **gw**, **kw**, **ky** are very common; `ts-` word-initially is highly Hausa-typical (*tsari*, *tsohuwa*, *tsakani*).
- Final **-a** on most feminine nouns and verbal nouns (*rana*, *sabuwa*, *tsari*, *aiki*).
- Plural endings **-una**, **-aye**, **-uka**, **-oCi**: *littafi → littattafai*, *gida → gidaje*.
- Orthographic `'y` / `ƴ` marks palatalised glottal (*'yan*, *'yar*).

## Morphology hooks

- Linker **-n** / **-r** (*gidan*, *motar*, *rigar*) is extremely frequent.
- PAC clitics **na ka kin ya ta mun kun sun** and continuous **ina kana yana muna suna** are a strong signature when they appear together before verbs.
- Verb grade endings in **-e**, **-a**, **-o**, **-u** on CVCV stems.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Standard Latin punctuation. Apostrophe for glottal stop is content-bearing, not punctuation. No capital letter on hooked consonants at word start unless sentence-initial; Ajami (Arabic) usage exists but is rare on the web.

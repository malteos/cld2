Karem Abdul-Jabbar (/ˈk ⁇ ri'm æbʒədu /kəˈriːm æbˈduːl dʒəˈbɑːr/ l dundon ˈbəŋr/ k /kəˈriːm æbˈduːl dʒəˈbɑːr/ -REEM aban-DOOL mjɪn-BAR; namundag Ferdinand Lewis Alcindor Jr.; Abril 16, 1947) sarong Amerikanong propesyonal na basketbolista na nagtogtog nin 20 kapanahonan sa National Basketball Association (NBI) para sa Milwaukee Bucks asin Los Lakes Angeles. saiyang karera bilang sentro, si Abdul-Jabbar sarong rekord anom-na-panahon na NBA Most Valuable Player (MVP).

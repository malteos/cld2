Yoweri Kaguta Museveni Tibuhaburwa (akazarwa 15|9|1944) Ni Omu nya Uganda Kandi omunya eby'obutegyekyi Kandi akahomura okukurira engabo z'eihanga Kandi Ni purezident was mwenda Kandi akiriho nayebembera Purezident wa Uganda. Yoweri Museveni[1] akeetaba omu ntaro nyingi ezayihireho bamwe aha engoma nka Obote na Amin ataka kwatsire bwebembezi omu mwaka 1986 . Museveni aka zaarwa ebiro 15 September 1944 abazeire be Mzee Amos Kaguta (1916–2013), Omutungi w'ente, and Esteri Kokundeka Nganzi (1918–2001), omuri Rukungiri. Ni OmuHima wa eihanga rya Mpororo hati eri emwe Ahari Ankole).
Obujurizi edit
Kurigiririra ahari Julius Nyerere, Eishe Museveni Amos Kaguta, akaba Ari omu nyangabo wa King's African Rifles omubwiire bwa orutaro really ensi yoona rwa kabiri . Akaba Ari omu Kakungu ka 7 kabarwani. Museveni kuyaziirwe,abanyabuzaare baguma nibagira ngu Eishe akaba Ari mu-seveni, aho niho yarugirire kutunga eiziina Museveni.
Obujurizi
Eeka ye ekeija ya furikira Ntungamo, obwiire obwo eri British Protectorate of Uganda. Museveni akashomera Kyamate Elementary School, Mbarara High School, na Ntare School ahabwa primary na secondary . Yashomeraho University of Dar es Salaam omuri Tanzania ahabwa ebyobwegyese bwa ahiguru. Akashoma entatsya nana obutegyeki. Obwo akiri aha university, akakoraho Ekibiina Kya abeegi ekibakweta [[Univers Kandi yayebembera abeegi ba FRELIMO omu Portuguese Mozambique kutunga obukugu omu mahe.

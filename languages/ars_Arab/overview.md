# Najdi Arabic (ars)

## Summary

Najdi Arabic (endonym: **اللهجة النجدية** *al-lahja n-najdiyya*) is the
Arabian-peninsula dialect centred on the Najd plateau of central Saudi
Arabia, including Riyadh. Family: Afro-Asiatic → Semitic → Central Semitic →
Arabic → Arabian Peninsula Arabic → Najdi. ISO: **639-3 `ars`** (under the
`ara` macrolanguage). Native speakers ~10–15 million, primarily in central
and northern Saudi Arabia (Riyadh, Qassim, Ha'il), with extensions into
Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, and parts of the UAE. Prestigious within Saudi
Arabia due to association with the royal family and Riyadh.

## Writing system

Uses the **Arabic script** (U+0600–U+06FF), conventionally with MSA
orthography. In informal writing, speakers sometimes spell the reflex of
Classical /q/ → [g] as **ق** (retained) or as **ك** / **گ** / **ج**, and
the reflexes of /dʒ/ (ج) may be written as **ي** to capture the Najdi
pronunciation (e.g. ريال → *riyal* vs dialect *ryāl*). No casing. Short
vowels unmarked.

## Status

Vehicular in the Saudi capital region and in broadcast media targeted at the
Gulf audience, though Saudi media often blends Najdi, Hejazi, and MSA. Online
use is extensive on X/Twitter, YouTube, and Snapchat, where CommonLID is
likely to encounter ars-labelled text.

# Najdi Arabic grammar

- **Word order**: SVO dominant, with VSO available for topic-focus structures.
  Bedouin-descended morphology with conservative features.
- **Morphology**: Root-and-pattern Arabic. Preservation of **gender distinction
  in 2pl and 3pl** verb/pronoun forms (انتو/انتن, هم/هن) — a conservative
  Bedouin trait. Indefinite marker **ـن (tanwīn)** sometimes preserved on
  indefinite nouns: بيتٍ كبير "a big house".
- **Nouns**: Masc/fem. Broken plurals dominate; sound plurals ـين / ـات. No
  case marking in speech, though vestigial tanwīn on indefinite noun + adj
  constructions is a Najdi hallmark.
- **Verbs**: Perfective vs imperfective. Future particle **ب-** (b- + impf,
  e.g. بيروح "he will go") or **راح / رح** + impf. No habitual prefix (unlike
  arz **بـ** or ary **كـ/تـ**). Pseudo-verbs expressing want: **يبي / أبي /
  تبي** "I/you want" (Gulf-wide, strongly Najdi).
- **Phonology**: /q/ → **[g]** (قال → *gāl*). /k/ may affricate to **[ts]**
  in some contexts (Classical /kalb/ → *tsalb* "dog") — *kaškaša/gahgaha*
  phenomena. Interdentals ث ذ ظ preserved (unlike arz/ary). Emphatics stable.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; no present copula. Interrogatives: **وش / ويش**
  "what", **وين** "where", **متى** "when", **ليش** "why", **كيف** "how",
  **مين / منو** "who". Negation **ما** (past/impf), **مو** / **موب** (non-
  verbal), **لا** (imperative). Demonstratives **هذا / هذي / هذوله** (closer
  to MSA than to Maghrebi هاد or Egyptian ده).

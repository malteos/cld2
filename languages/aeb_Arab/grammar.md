# Tunisian Arabic grammar

- **Word order**: SVO. Synthetic Semitic morphology. Strong syncope of short
  vowels producing initial consonant clusters (*ktib* "he wrote", *nktib*
  "I/we write").
- **Morphology**: Root-and-pattern. Maghrebi 1sg/1pl innovation: impf 1sg has
  prefix **نـ** and impf 1pl adds suffix **ـوا** so that 1sg and 1pl are
  formally distinguished (نكتب "I write" vs نكتبوا "we write"). No
  habitual/progressive prefix (unlike ary **كـ** or arz **بـ**). Object
  pronoun suffixes attach to verbs and nouns.
- **Nouns**: Masc/fem (ة preserved). Analytic genitive particle **متاع / تاع**
  "of" coexists with construct state: الكتاب متاعي "my book". Plurals broken
  dominant, sound ـين / ـات.
- **Verbs**: Perfective vs imperfective. Bare imperfective is present
  indicative (no habitual prefix). Future **باش / بش** + impf (باش نمشي "I
  will go"). Progressive marked by active participle **قاعد + impf** (قاعد
  نكتب "I am writing"). Negation **ما...ش**: ماجيتش, ماكلتش.
- **Phonology**: /q/ preserved as **[q]** in most of the north (contrasts with
  **[g]** in Bedouin-influenced areas). Interdentals ث ذ ظ generally merged
  with t/d/ḍ. Short-vowel syncope heavy. Heavy use of /p/, /v/, /g/ in
  borrowings.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; no present copula. Interrogatives: **شنوة / شنيّة
  / آش** (what), **وين** (where), **وقتاش / إمتى** (when), **علاش** (why),
  **كيفاش** (how), **شكون** (who). Demonstratives: **هذا / هذي / هذوم** or
  Maghrebi-style **هاذا / هاذي**.

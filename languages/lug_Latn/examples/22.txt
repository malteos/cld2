Goolo ye ey'okubiri gye yateebye ekiro kya leero yeyawezezza goolo ye 60 mu sizoni, era ye musambi asoose okuweza goolo 60 mu sizoni emu okuva mu 1995-1996, Jaromir Jagr nne Mario Lemieux buli omu bwe yakikola. Batten bamulinyisa mu kifo kya 190th mu 2008 mu bamerica ebikumi 400 abasinga obuggaga ebibalirirwa mu $2.3 billion. Yattikirwa mu kolegi ya sayansi nebyokusiiga mu ssetendekero lya Virginia mu 1950 era yali mugabi wabuyambi wankukunala mu kitongole ekyo. Ekkomera lya Abu Gharib lyakolezebwaako omuliro nga bekalakasa.

# Source: Wikipedia article "Kkanisa ya Yeso Eyannamaddala" (https://lg.wikipedia.org/)

Mwoyo Mutukuvu: "Receiving the Holy Spirit, evidenced by speaking in tongues, is the guarantee of our inheritance of the Kingdom of Heaven". Okubatizibwa: "Water baptism is the sacrament for the remission of sins and for regeneration. The baptism takes place in natural living water, such as the river, sea, or spring. The Baptist, whom already has received baptism of water and the Holy Spirit, conducts the baptism in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ.

Ng’amagye g’abamerika gamaze okutwala obuyinza eri ekomera elyo, kyazulibwa nti abasibe bali batulugunyizibwa nyo, era nelimanyika nyo olwekyo. Piquet Jr. yafuna akabenje mumpaka ze motoka eza Singapore 2008, oluvanyuma lwa Fernando Alonso okuyimirira okukyusa emipiira; emotoka enkuumi kwekuyingira wo. Emotoka ezaali mu maaso ga Alonso bwe zagenda okunywa amafuta mu motoka yokwekumisa, yeyongeddeyo mu maaso okulya obuwanguzi. Piquet Jr yagobebwa oluvanyuma lw’emisinde ejja 2009 ejja Hungarian Grand Prix.

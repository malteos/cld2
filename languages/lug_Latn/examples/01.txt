# Source: Wikipedia article "Kampala" (https://lg.wikipedia.org/)

Kampala kye kibuga kya Uganda ekikulu. Ekibuga kirimu abantu 1,208,544 era kye kisinga obunene mu Yuganda. Kampala erimu ggombolola ttaano: Central, Kawempe, Makindye, Nakawa ne Rubaga.
Kampala ky'ekibuga ekikulu ekya ensi Yuganda. Kyatandikira ku busozi musanvu naye nga olwaleero kigaziye nekituuka ku busozi obuwerako. Kifugibwa omukulembeze alondebwa obuteerevu nga ekifo kye kiyitibwa "lord Mayor". Meeya aliko ye Ssalongo Lukwago Elias.

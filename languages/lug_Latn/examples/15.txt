Ekkuumiro ly'ebisolo erya Rwenzori Mountains National Park lyatandikibwawo mu 1991. Lyakakasibwa UNESCO ng'ekifo eky'obulambuzi mu 1994 olw'obulungi bw'endabika bwalyo ey'obutonde. Abayeekera beefunza ekkuumiro ly'ebisolo erya Rwenzori Mountains okuva mu 1997 okutuuka Ssebaaseka 2001. Ekkuumiro lyateekebwa ku lukalala lwa UNESCO olw'ensi yonna olw'ebifo by'obulambuzi ebyali mu katyaba wakati wa 1999 ne 2004 olw'obutaali butebenkevu n'ebbula ly'ebikozesebwa mu kkuumiro. because of insecurity and a lack of resources in the park.

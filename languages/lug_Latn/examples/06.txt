# Source: Wikipedia article "Kkanisa ya Yeso Eyannamaddala" (https://lg.wikipedia.org/)

Okunaaza bigere: "The sacrament of feet washing enables one to have a part with the Lord Jesus. It also serves as a constant reminder that one should have love, holiness, humility, forgiveness and service. Every person who has received water baptism must have their feet washed in the name of Jesus Christ. Mutual feet washing may be practiced whenever is appropriate".

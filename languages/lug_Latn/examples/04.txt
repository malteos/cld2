Omulembe gwaffe guno gwetulimu era abawahaab bagenze wala nnyo mukusaasaanya enkola yaabwe mubusiramu naye nga bawagirwa na Saud okuva mumagoba ga petrol. Era baateekawo ekigero eky’amafuta nga kyeyawulidde kukulaakulanya madhihab ga bawahaab nakugasaasaanya Mubasiram. Mubufuufu, singa sissente ezo empitirivu, abasiramu balibadde tebafuna buzibu bwabwe.

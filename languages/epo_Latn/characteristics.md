# Esperanto Identification Characteristics

## Unique Diacritic Characters

Esperanto uses six letters with diacritics that are highly diagnostic for language identification:

| Character | Base | Diacritic | IPA | Notes |
|-----------|------|-----------|-----|-------|
| **ĉ** | c | circumflex | /tʃ/ | like English "ch" in "church" |
| **ĝ** | g | circumflex | /dʒ/ | like English "j" in "judge" |
| **ĥ** | h | circumflex | /x/ | like Scottish "ch" in "loch" (rare) |
| **ĵ** | j | circumflex | /ʒ/ | like "s" in "measure" |
| **ŝ** | s | circumflex | /ʃ/ | like English "sh" in "ship" |
| **ŭ** | u | breve | /w/ | semivowel, only in diphthongs aŭ, eŭ |

The combination of circumflex accents on c, g, h, j, s and the breve on u is unique to Esperanto among widely used languages. The presence of even one or two of these characters (especially ĉ, ĝ, or ŝ) in a text is a strong indicator of Esperanto. The character ĥ is becoming rare in modern usage, with many speakers preferring alternatives (e.g., *kemio* instead of *ĥemio* for "chemistry").

In computing contexts, when the diacritics are unavailable, the "x-system" (cx, gx, hx, jx, sx, ux) or the older "h-system" (ch, gh, hh, jh, sh, u) may be used as substitutes.

## Common Bigrams and Trigrams

### Highly Diagnostic Bigrams
- **as** (present tense ending: estas, havas, faras)
- **is** (past tense ending: estis, havis, faris)
- **os** (future tense ending: estos, havos, faros)
- **aj** (plural adjective ending: bonaj, grandaj)
- **oj** (plural noun ending: domoj, homoj)
- **an** (common in roots and accusative: granda, homan)
- **on** (accusative nouns: domon, libron)
- **in** (various: patrino, vino, lingvo)
- **en** (preposition "in" + common in words)
- **la** (definite article, extremely frequent)
- **de** (preposition "of/from")
- **ĉi** (proximity particle + correlative prefix)
- **aŭ** (conjunction "or" + diphthong)

### Common Trigrams
- **kaj** (conjunction "and" - one of the most frequent words)
- **est** (root of "esti" - to be: estas, estis, estos)
- **ant** (present participle: lernanta, parolanta)
- **int** (past participle: lerninta, parolinta)
- **ita** (passive past participle: lernita, farita)
- **igi** (causative suffix: purigi, beligi)
- **iĝi** (inchoative suffix: ruĝiĝi, pliboniĝi)
- **ejo** (place suffix: lernejo, laborejo)

## Morphological Patterns

Esperanto's completely regular morphology creates highly distinctive patterns:

### Word Endings
- **All nouns** end in **-o** (singular) or **-oj** (plural): *domo, domoj, lingvo, lingvoj*
- **All adjectives** end in **-a** (singular) or **-aj** (plural): *bona, bonaj, granda, grandaj*
- **All adverbs** end in **-e**: *bone, rapide, feliĉe, certe*
- **Infinitive verbs** end in **-i**: *lerni, paroli, manĝi, dormi*
- **Present tense** always **-as**: *lernas, parolas, manĝas, dormas*
- **Past tense** always **-is**: *lernis, parolis, manĝis, dormis*
- **Future tense** always **-os**: *lernos, parolos, manĝos, dormos*
- **Conditional** always **-us**: *lernus, parolus, manĝus, dormus*
- **Imperative** always **-u**: *lernu, parolu, manĝu, dormu*
- **Accusative** always adds **-n**: *domon, bonajn, librojn, min, lin*

### Plural + Accusative Combinations
The combinations **-ojn** (plural accusative nouns) and **-ajn** (plural accusative adjectives) are nearly unique to Esperanto and are strong identification markers: *bonajn librojn* (good books, as direct object).

## Regular Word Building System

The systematic compounding of roots with affixes is highly distinctive. A single root can generate dozens of related words:

From **san-** (health):
- sano (health), sana (healthy), sane (healthily)
- malsano (illness), malsana (ill), malsane (unhealthily)
- sanigi (to heal), malsanigi (to make ill)
- saniĝi (to recover), malsaniĝi (to fall ill)
- sanigilo (remedy), malsanulejo (hospital)
- sanulo (healthy person), malsanulo (sick person)

This productive system means that Esperanto texts contain many long, morphologically complex words with recognizable affixes, creating a distinctive textual fingerprint for language identification.

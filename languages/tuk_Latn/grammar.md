# Turkmen grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV. Modifier–head throughout
  (adjective–noun, genitive–noun, relative–noun). Free-ish order
  owing to case marking.
- **Morphology**: Highly agglutinative with transparent suffix
  boundaries. Nine-vowel system (front/back × rounded/unrounded,
  plus phonemically long vowels) with backness and roundness
  harmony regulating allomorphs.
- **Nouns**: No gender, no article. Six cases: nominative (∅),
  accusative *-(n)y/-(n)i*, dative *-a/-e*, locative *-da/-de*,
  ablative *-dan/-den*, genitive *-yň/-iň/-uň/-üň*. Plural
  *-lar/-ler*. Possessive suffixes precede case: *-ym/-im/-um/-üm*
  etc.
- **Verbs**: Suffix chain: negation *-ma/-me*, ability *-yp bil-*,
  tense/aspect (*-dy/-di* past, *-ypdyr/-ipdir* evidential,
  *-ýar/-ýär* progressive/present, *-ar/-er* aorist, *-jak/-jek*
  future), personal endings. Copula clitic *-dyr/-dir/-dur/-dür*.
- **Phonology**: Eight-to-nine vowels plus **phonemic vowel length**
  (*aat* "horse" vs *at* "name"; length often spelled with doubled
  vowels). Consonants include /ŋ/ written `ň` and /ʒ/ written `ž`,
  plus bilabial /w/ written `w`. Stress is typically word-final.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. No overt present-tense copula. Suffixal
  negation; polar questions via the enclitic *-my/-mi*. Relative
  clauses are participial.

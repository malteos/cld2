The Century Dictionary is “designed to be a practically complete record of the main body of English speech, from the time of the mingling of the Old French and Anglo-Saxon to the present day”, including “technical terms of the various sciences, arts, trades, and professions”. It offers “definitions proper of such related encyclopedic matter” with the addition of “pictorial illustrations”.

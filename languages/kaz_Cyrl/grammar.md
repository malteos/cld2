# Kazakh Grammar / Қазақ тілінің грамматикасы

## Word Order

Kazakh follows a strict **SOV (Subject-Object-Verb)** word order as its default syntactic pattern. The verb typically appears at the end of the sentence, with the subject preceding the object. Modifiers precede the elements they modify: adjectives come before nouns, adverbs before verbs, and relative clauses before head nouns.

Example:
- **Мен кітап оқыдым.** (Men kitap oqydym.) — "I read a book." (Subject-Object-Verb)
- **Үлкен қала** (Ulken qala) — "Big city" (Adjective-Noun)

While SOV is the canonical order, word order can be varied for emphasis or topicalization. Placing an element immediately before the verb gives it focus, while fronting an element to the beginning of the sentence marks it as the topic.

## Agglutinative Morphology

Kazakh is a highly **agglutinative** language, meaning that grammatical relationships are expressed by adding suffixes to word stems in a regular, sequential manner. A single word can carry multiple suffixes, each expressing a distinct grammatical function. The suffixes attach in a fixed order: stem + plural + possessive + case + other particles.

Example: **үйлерімізде** (üylerimizde) — "in our houses"
- үй (house) + лер (plural) + іміз (our, 1st person plural possessive) + де (locative case)

This agglutinative structure means that Kazakh words can be significantly longer than their English equivalents, and a single word may encode information that requires an entire phrase in analytic languages.

## Vowel Harmony

Kazakh exhibits a robust system of **vowel harmony**, which is a defining feature of Turkic languages. Vowel harmony requires that the vowels in suffixes agree with the vowels in the stem in terms of frontness/backness and sometimes rounding.

Kazakh vowels are divided into:
- **Back vowels:** а, о, ұ, ы
- **Front vowels:** ә, ө, ү, і, е

If the last vowel of the stem is a back vowel, the suffix must contain back vowels. If the last vowel is a front vowel, the suffix must contain front vowels. This principle governs nearly all suffix selection in the language.

Examples:
- **қала + да = қалада** ("in the city" — back harmony)
- **үй + де = үйде** ("in the house" — front harmony)
- **бала + лар = балалар** ("children" — back harmony plural)
- **үй + лер = үйлер** ("houses" — front harmony plural)

## Noun System

### Cases

Kazakh has **seven grammatical cases**, each marked by suffixes that vary according to vowel harmony and the final sound of the stem:

1. **Атау септік (Nominative):** Unmarked, indicates the subject. *Бала* — "the child"
2. **Ілік септік (Genitive):** Marks possession. Suffixes: -ның/-нің, -дың/-дің, -тың/-тің. *Баланың* — "of the child"
3. **Барыс септік (Dative-Directional):** Indicates direction or recipient. Suffixes: -ға/-ге, -қа/-ке. *Балаға* — "to the child"
4. **Табыс септік (Accusative):** Marks the definite direct object. Suffixes: -ны/-ні, -ды/-ді, -ты/-ті. *Баланы* — "the child (object)"
5. **Жатыс септік (Locative):** Indicates location. Suffixes: -да/-де, -та/-те. *Балада* — "at the child"
6. **Шығыс септік (Ablative):** Indicates source or origin. Suffixes: -дан/-ден, -тан/-тен, -нан/-нен. *Баладан* — "from the child"
7. **Көмектес септік (Instrumental):** Indicates the means or companion. Suffixes: -мен/-бен/-пен. *Баламен* — "with the child"

### Number

Kazakh nouns are unmarked for singular. The plural is formed with the suffix **-лар/-лер/-дар/-дер/-тар/-тер**, selected according to vowel harmony and the final consonant of the stem:

- After vowels and voiced consonants (back): **-лар** (*балалар* — "children")
- After vowels and voiced consonants (front): **-лер** (*үйлер* — "houses")
- After voiced stops and nasals (back): **-дар** (*қыздар* — "girls")
- After voiced stops and nasals (front): **-дер** (*көздер* — "eyes")
- After voiceless consonants (back): **-тар** (*кітаптар* — "books")
- After voiceless consonants (front): **-тер** (*білімгерлер* — "scholars")

### No Grammatical Gender

Kazakh has **no grammatical gender**. There are no masculine, feminine, or neuter categories. Nouns, pronouns, adjectives, and verbs do not inflect for gender. Natural gender is expressed lexically where needed (e.g., *ата* "grandfather" vs. *әже* "grandmother").

### Possessive Suffixes

Possessive relationships are marked by suffixes attached to the possessed noun:
- 1st singular: **-м/-ым/-ім** (*кітабым* — "my book")
- 2nd singular (informal): **-ң/-ың/-ің** (*кітабың* — "your book")
- 2nd singular (formal): **-ңыз/-ңіз** (*кітабыңыз* — "your book")
- 3rd singular: **-сы/-сі/-ы/-і** (*кітабы* — "his/her book")
- 1st plural: **-мыз/-міз** (*кітабымыз* — "our book")
- 2nd plural: **-ларың/-лерің** (*кітаптарың* — "your books")
- 3rd plural: **-лары/-лері** (*кітаптары* — "their books")

## Verb System

### Verb Stems and Infinitive

The Kazakh verb infinitive is formed with the suffix **-у/-ю** (e.g., *оқу* — "to read," *жазу* — "to write," *келу* — "to come").

### Tenses

Kazakh has a complex tense system with multiple past, present, and future forms:

- **Definite past (жедел өткен шақ):** -ды/-ді/-ты/-ті. *Мен оқыдым* — "I read (and finished)"
- **Indefinite past (бұрынғы өткен шақ):** -ған/-ген/-қан/-кен. *Мен оқыған* — "I have read / apparently read"
- **Present continuous (ауыспалы осы шақ):** -а/-е/-й + auxiliary *жатыр/тұр/отыр/жүр*. *Мен оқып жатырмын* — "I am reading"
- **Habitual present (ауыспалы осы шақ):** -а/-е/-й + personal endings. *Мен оқимын* — "I read (habitually)"
- **Future intentional (болжалды келер шақ):** -мақ/-мек + possessive. *Мен оқымақпын* — "I intend to read"
- **Simple future (мақсатты келер шақ):** -а/-е/-й + personal endings or auxiliary forms. *Мен оқырмын* — "I will read"

### Moods

- **Indicative (Ашық рай):** The default mood for statements of fact.
- **Imperative (Бұйрық рай):** Formed from the bare stem for 2nd person informal. *Оқы!* — "Read!"
- **Optative/Desiderative (Қалау рай):** Expresses wish or desire. *Оқысам* — "If I read / I wish to read"
- **Conditional (Шартты рай):** -са/-се + personal endings. *Оқысам* — "If I read"

### Voice

Kazakh verbs can take voice suffixes:
- **Passive:** -ыл/-іл, -л. *Жазылды* — "was written"
- **Causative:** -дыр/-дір, -тыр/-тір, -ғыз/-гіз, -қыз/-кіз. *Оқытты* — "made (someone) read / taught"
- **Reflexive:** -ын/-ін. *Жуынды* — "washed himself"
- **Reciprocal:** -ыс/-іс. *Көрісті* — "met each other (saw each other)"

### Negation

Verbal negation is formed with the suffix **-ма/-ме/-ба/-бе/-па/-пе** inserted before tense markers:
- *Оқымадым* — "I did not read"
- *Келмейді* — "He/she does not come"

### Converbs and Participles

Kazakh makes extensive use of converbs (verbal adverbs) and participles:
- **-ып/-іп/-п** (sequential converb): *Келіп отырды* — "Having come, sat down"
- **-ғанда/-генде** (temporal participle): *Келгенде* — "When (he/she) came"
- **-атын/-етін** (habitual participle): *Оқитын бала* — "The child who reads"

## Syntax

### Postpositions

Kazakh uses **postpositions** rather than prepositions. Postpositions follow the noun they govern and typically require the noun to be in a specific case:

- **үшін** (for): *Сенің үшін* — "for you"
- **туралы** (about): *Қазақстан туралы* — "about Kazakhstan"
- **арқылы** (through): *Интернет арқылы* — "through the Internet"
- **бойынша** (according to): *Заң бойынша* — "according to the law"
- **кейін** (after): *Сабақтан кейін* — "after class"
- **бұрын** (before): *Кештен бұрын* — "before evening"

### Relative Clauses

Kazakh does not use relative pronouns. Instead, relative clauses are formed using participial constructions that precede the head noun:
- *Мен оқыған кітап* — "The book that I read" (literally: "I read book")

### Question Formation

Questions are formed by adding the particle **ма/ме/ба/бе/па/пе** to the verb or by using question words such as *кім* (who), *не* (what), *қайда* (where), *қашан* (when), *қалай* (how), *неге/неліктен* (why).

### Copula

In the present tense, Kazakh often omits the copula (the verb "to be"). Identity and description are expressed through juxtaposition or with personal predicative suffixes:
- *Мен студентпін* — "I am a student" (-пін is the predicative suffix)
- *Ол мұғалім* — "He/she is a teacher" (zero copula in 3rd person)

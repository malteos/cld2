# Differences (zul)

## Confusable languages

### zul vs xho (Xhosa)

Zulu and Xhosa are mutually intelligible Nguni cousins and are the
hardest pair in this sub-set.

- Both use clicks `c/q/x`; **xho has higher click density** and
  more click+h combinations (`tsh`, `tyh`, `ngc`, `nkq`).
- **`hl` / `dl` are far more frequent in xho** (`ukuhla`, `dlala`,
  `umhlobo`) than in zul.
- Function words: zul `futhi`, `kodwa`, `ngoba`, `ukuthi`, `uma` vs
  xho `kwaye`, `kodwa`, `ngenxa yokuba`, `ukuba`, `xa`.
- Copulas: zul `ngu-` / `yi-`; xho often `ngu-`, `ngumlo-`,
  `ngaba-`.
- Subject concord 1sg: zul `ngi-`; xho `ndi-` (strong cue —
  `ndiyabulela` is Xhosa, `ngiyabonga` is Zulu).

### zul vs other Bantu in the set

- vs **sna/swh/swa/lin/nso/sot/kik/lug/nyn**: only zul and xho use
  the click letters `c/q/x` inside native roots — presence of
  `-cela-`, `-qala-`, `-xola-` strongly narrows to Nguni.
- vs **nso/sot**: zul has `hl/dl` (Sotho has `hl` but little `dl`),
  and uses augment vowels, which Sotho lacks.

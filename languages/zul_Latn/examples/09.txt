Amadokhumenti aveze amabhangi ayishumi nane asize amakhasimende acebile ukuba afihle izigidigidi zama-dollar ase-Melika zengcebo ukugwema intela kanye neminye imithetho. Iphephandaba laseBhrithani iThe Guardian lisikisele ukuthi iDeutsche Bank yayilawula cishe ingxenye yesithathu yezinkampani eziseceleni ezingu-1200 ezisetshenziselwe ukufeza lokhu. Bekunokubhikisha emhlabeni jikelele, ukumangalelwa kobugebengu okuningana, futhi abaholi bahulumeni wase-Iceland nasePakistan basula ezikhundleni bobabili.

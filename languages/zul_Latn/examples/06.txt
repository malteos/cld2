I-JAS 39C Gripen yashayeka emgwaqweni okugijima kuwo indiza cishe ngabo-9:30 ekuseni ngesikhathi sendawo (02:30 UTC) yaqhuma, okwavala izindiza zabantu bonke esikhumulweni sezindiza. Umshayeli webhanoyi wahlonzwa njengoMholi we-Squadron u-Dilokrit Pattavee. Abezindaba bendawo babika ukuthi imoto yomlilo yasesikhumulweni sezindiza yaginqika ngenkathi iphuthuma. UVidal oneminyaka engu-28 wayejoyine i-Barça kumasizini amathathu adlule, esuka e-Sevilla. Kusukela ngenkathi ethuthele enhlokodolobha yase-Catalan, uVidal udlalele iqembu imidlalo engu-49.

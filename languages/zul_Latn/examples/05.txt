NgoMsombuluko, ososayensi base-Stanford University School of Medicine bamemezele ithuluzi Elisha elikwazi ukuhlukanisa amagqamuzane ngezinhlobo zawo: ucezwana oluncane olunyathelisekayo olungakhandwa ngokusebenzisa imishini evamile yokunyathelisa ngokusesilinganisweni ngesenti elilodwa laseMelika. Abacwaningi abahola phambili bathi lokhu kungaholela ekushesheni kutholeke umhlavuza, isifo sofuba, igciwane lesandulela ngculazi kanye nomalaleveva ezigulini ezisemazweni ampofu, lapho amazinga okusinda ezifweni ezinjengomdlavuza webele angaba nguhhafuwamazwe acebile.

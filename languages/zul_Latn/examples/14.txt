Isiqephu ngasinye sohlelo sizogxila esihlokweni sencwadi ethile bese sihlola leso sihloko ngezindaba eziningana. Uhlelo ngalunye luzophinde lunikeze izincomo zezincwadi izingane okufanele  zizibheke uma ziya emtapolwazi wazo. UJohn Grant, osuka wase-WNED Buffalo (isiteshi sasekhaya seReading Rainbow) uthe "iReading Rainbow ifundise izingane ukuthi kungani kufanele zifunde,… uthando lokufunda — [uhlelo] lukhuthaze izingane ukuba zithathe incwadi zifunde." Abanye bakholelwa, kuhlanganise noJohn Grant, ukuthi ukusweleka okukhulu kwemali kanye nokushintsha kwefilosofi yezinhlelo zemfundo zethelevishini kube nomthelela ekupheleni kwezinhlelo ezilandelanayo.

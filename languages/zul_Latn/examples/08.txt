Umlando wokuncintisana phakathi kukaNadal nomuntu waseKhanada u-7–2. Usanda kwehluleka muva nje ebhekene no-Raonic eBrisbane Open. UNadal uthole amaphuzu angu-88% ahlangene emaphuzwini angu-76 okuwina umdlalo engxenyeni yokuqala. Ngemva komdlalo, iNkosi Yesihlabathi ithe, "Ngijabulele nje ukubuya emizuliswaneni yokugcina yezenzakalo ezibaluleke kakhulu. Ngilapha ukuzozama ukunqoba lokhu." "I-Panama Papers" igama eliyisambulela lamadokhumenti cishe ayizigidi eziyishumi avela enkampanini yabameli asePanama i-Mossack Fonseca, akhishwa kwabezindaba entwasahlobo ka-2016.

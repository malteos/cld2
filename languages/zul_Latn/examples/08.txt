# Source: https://www.isolezwe.co.za/ (Isolezwe — Zulu daily)

“Njengoba sizibekela imigomo okufanele siyifeze njengeqembu kufanele sigxile kithina, siphinde sibheke nemidlalo ngokuza kwayo. Asigxilile kwabanye abantu nokuthi amanye amaqembu enzani. Asifuni ukuthembela kwabanye, sidinga ukwenza lokhu okuzosisiza sizuze lokhu esifisa ukukuzuza,” kusho uZwane.

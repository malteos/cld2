# Source: Wikipedia article "IRiphabhuliki yaseNingizimu Afrika" (https://zu.wikipedia.org/)

Zikhona izinhlangano zepolitiki ezahlanganyela nokhongolose ezifana ne-SACP (amakomanisi), i-PAC kanye nezinye ezifana no-BCM. Kwahamba kanjalo eNingizimu Afrika kwaze kwaboshwa osopolitiki ngaleso sikhathi sengcindezelo abafana no Nelson Mandela, Walter Sisulu, Govan Mbeki, Raymond Mhlaba, Robert Sobukwe, Andrew Mlangeni, Dennis Goldberg,Jeffrey Masemola, Kasrils nabanye. Kanti abanye baya ekudingisweni abafana no-Oliver Tambo, Thabo Mbeki, Chris Hani, Duma Nokwe, Moses Kotane.

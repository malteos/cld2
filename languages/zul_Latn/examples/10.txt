Ezalelwe eHong Kong, u-Ma wafunda eNyuvesi yaseNew York naseSikoleni soMthetho sase-Havard futhi wabuye waba “nomazisi” waseMelika wokuba umhlali wasikhathi sonke. UHsieh wasikisela phakathi nokhetho ukuthi uMa angabaleka ezweni phakathi nenkathi yenhlekelele. U-Hsieh uphinde waphikisa ukuthi uMa othandekayo ezithombeni wayesizwa yisitayela kunengqikithi. Naphezu kwezinsolo, uMa waphumelela enkundleni yokukhankasela ubudlelwane obuseduze nezwekazi laseShayina. Umdlali Wosuku wanamuhla ngu-Alex Ovechkin weWashington Capitals.

Ukubhikisha kuqale cishe ngo-11:00 isikhathi sendawo (UTC+1) eWhitehall ebhekene nendawo yokungena eDowning Street egadwe amaphoyisa, indawo engokomthetho kaNdunankulu. Ngemuva nje kuka-11:00, ababhikishi bavimbe izimoto ezibheke enyakatho e-Whitehall. Ngo-11:20, amaphoyisa acele ababhikishi ukuthi bahlehlele endaweni yabahamba ngezinyawo, bethi kudingeka balinganisele phakathi kwelungelo lokubhikisha kanye nesiminyaminya esakhekayo. Ngabo-11:29 ababhikishi baya e-Whitehall, badlula iTrafalgar Square, ngaseStrand, badlula ngase-Aldwych benyuka eKingsway bebheke eHolborn lapho iConservative Party lalibambe iSpring Forum ehhotela eGrand Connaught Rooms.

UPiquet Jr. waxoshwa ngemva kwe-Grand Prix yase-Hungary ka-2009. Ngo-8:46 ngqo ekuseni kwathuleka cwaka kulo lonke idolobha, okubonisa umzuzu oqondile indiza yokuqala ishaye inhloso yayo. Imisebe yokukhanya emibili yanyuka ukuze ibheke emkhathini phakathi nobusuku. Umsebenzi wokwakha amabhilidi amahlanu amade uyaqhubeka endaweni yesakhiwo, nendawo yokuthutha kanye nendawo yesikhumbuzo phakathi nendawo. Umboniso we-PBS lunemiklomelo ye-Emmy engaphezu kwamashumi amabili nane futhi iboniswa isikhathi esifushane kune-Sesame Street neMister Rogers’s Neighborhood kuphela.

# Czech grammar

- **Word order**: Default SVO; free in practice because of rich case marking,
  with end-focus informing surface order. Fusional and synthetic.
- **Morphology**: Fusional with extensive suffixation. Productive derivation
  via `-ost` (abstract nouns), `-ník/-ařík` (agent nouns), `-ský/-cký`
  (relational adjectives), `-ovat` (verbalisers).
- **Nouns**: Three genders (masculine — split into animate and inanimate —
  feminine, neuter) and two numbers. Seven cases: nominative, genitive,
  dative, accusative, vocative, locative, instrumental. Paradigms labelled
  by model nouns: **pán, hrad, muž, stroj** (masc.), **žena, růže, píseň,
  kost** (fem.), **město, moře, kuře, stavení** (neut.). The vocative is
  productive (`pane Novák → pane Nováku!`).
- **Verbs**: Imperfective/perfective aspect; present-tense endings `-m/-u,
  -š, -Ø/-e, -me, -te, -jí/-ou`. Past tense with **l-participle** plus
  auxiliary `být`: `psal jsem, psala jsi, psali jsme`. Future of
  imperfectives with `budu + inf` (`budu psát`); perfectives use simple
  non-past forms for future (`napíšu`). Conditional with `bych, bys, by,
  bychom, byste, by` + l-participle. Imperative in `-ø/-i/-ej` plus 1pl/2pl.
- **Phonology**: Long/short vowel distinction (á é í ó ú ů ý). Consonants
  include unique /r̝/ (ř), palatals /c ɟ ɲ/ (ť ď ň), and alveolo-palatal
  sibilant /ʃ ʒ/ (š ž). Syllabic /r̩/ and /l̩/ in words like `vlk, prst, krk`.
  Stress is fixed on the first syllable, independent of vowel length.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop; no articles. Present copula `být` (`jsem, jsi, je,
  jsme, jste, jsou`) is obligatory. Negation with prefix `ne-` glued to the
  verb (`nemám, nevím, nerozumím`). Reflexive/passive clitic `se` / dative
  `si` is a second-position enclitic.

# Grammar (xho)

- **Word order**: SVO, prepositional, head-initial NPs
  (`umntu omhle` "a beautiful person"). Pro-drop via subject concord.
- **Morphology**: agglutinative, prefixing, conjunctive orthography —
  full clauses are written as single words (`ndiyakuthanda` "I love
  you").
- **Nouns**: 15 noun classes with singular/plural pairs. Prefixes:
  `um(u)-/aba-`, `um(u)-/imi-`, `i(li)-/ama-`, `isi-/izi-`,
  `i(n)-/i(z)in-`, `u(lu)-/i(z)in-`, `ubu-`, `uku-`. The **augment
  vowel** (`a-`, `i-`, `u-`) sits before the class prefix in most
  syntactic positions.
- **Agreement**: full concord through subject, object, adjectives,
  possessives, demonstratives and copulas.
- **Verbs**: 1sg subject concord **`ndi-`** (contrast Zulu `ngi-`);
  2sg `u-`; TAM markers `-ya-` (present disjoint), `-a-/-e-` (recent
  past), `-za ku-` / `-zo-` (future), `-sa-` (still), `-ke-` (ever),
  `-nga-` (potential). Negation `a-…-i`, `-nga-…-i`. Final vowel
  `-a` / subjunctive `-e`.
- **Phonology**: five vowels; three click places each with ~5
  laryngeal variants; phonemic tone (high/low) but not written;
  penultimate length. Xhosa's click inventory is one of the largest
  outside Khoisan.
- **Syntax**: copulas `ngu-`, `yi-`, `ngaba-` selected by class;
  possessive `-a` concord; locatives with `e-…-eni`; relative clauses
  use the `-yo` or `a-` relative marker.

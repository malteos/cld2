# Differences (xho)

## Confusable languages

### xho vs zul (Zulu)

The Nguni pair is the hardest in this sub-set; they share most
vocabulary and all morphology.

- **1sg concord** is the single most reliable cue: xho **`ndi-`**,
  zul `ngi-` (`ndicela` vs `ngicela`, `ndiyabonga` vs
  `ngiyabonga`).
- **Click density** is higher in xho; trigraphs `ngc`, `ngq`, `ngx`
  and the `tsh`, `tyh` sequences are far more common in xho.
- **`hl` / `dl`** are more frequent in xho (`hlala`, `dlala`,
  `umhlobo`, `kuhle`).
- Function words: xho `kwaye`, `ukuba`, `xa`, `okanye` vs zul
  `futhi`, `ukuthi`, `uma`, `noma`.
- Copulas: xho `ngu-`, `yi-`, `ngaba-`; zul `ngu-`, `yi-`, less
  `ngaba-`.

### xho vs other Bantu in the set

- vs **swh/swa/sna/lin/nso/sot/kik/lug/nyn**: only zul and xho
  contain the click letters `c/q/x` inside native roots. Presence
  of `-cela-`, `-qhuba-`, `-xela-` essentially pins the text to
  Nguni.
- vs **sot/nso**: xho has augments (`u-`, `a-`, `i-` before class
  prefix), clicks, and conjunctive spelling that Sotho-Tswana
  languages lack.

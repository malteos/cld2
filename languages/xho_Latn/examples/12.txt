# Source: https://www.jw.org/xh/ (jw.org Xhosa edition)

Umntu okufundelayo uza kukunceda ufunde izinto ezifundiswa yiBhayibhile, ngokwemixholo. Kule ncoko ifundisa ngeBhayibhile yencwadi ethi Phila Ubomi Obumnandi Ngonaphakade! uza kufunda ngomyalezo weBhayibhile nendlela onokukunceda ngayo. Ukuze ufunde nezinye izinto, bukela le vidiyo okanye ujonge eminye imibuzo abantu abaqhele ukuyibuza ngesi sifundo.

Ijele yase-Iraq eyi-Abu Ghraib iye yatshiswa ngoxa bekukho uqhankqalazo. Ijele yabanenkohlakalo emva kokufumaniseka kokuxhatshazwa kwebanjwa emva koko umkhosi waseMerika wayithathela kuyo. UPiquet Jr. utshayisile kw Grand Prix ka-2008 yase Singapore kamsinya nje emva kokba uFernando Alonso eye wathatha i-pit stop, ekhupha imoto yokhuseleko. Njengokuba ezinye iimoto ezaziphambhili ko-Alonso zayothela amafutha emvakwemoto yenkuseleko, wabagqitha wanqoba. UPiquet Jr. waye wagxothwa emva kweGrand Prix yaseHungary ka-2009.

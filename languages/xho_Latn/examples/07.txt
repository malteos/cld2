# Source: https://www.jw.org/xh/ (jw.org Xhosa edition)

Sineemvelaphi ezahlukahlukeneyo, kodwa sinosukelo olufanayo. Eyona nto ibalulekileyo sifuna ukungcwalisa uYehova , uThixo ekuthethwa ngaye eBhayibhileni nonguMdali wezinto zonke. Sizama ngamandla ethu onke ukuxelisa uYesu Kristu yaye sinelunda ngokuba ngamaKristu . Ngamnye kuthi usoloko echitha ixesha enceda abantu bafunde iBhayibhile nangoBukumkani bukaThixo . Ngenxa yokuba singqinela okanye sithetha ngoYehova uThixo nangoBukumkani bakhe, saziwa njengamaNgqina kaYehova .

Ubukumkani baseSkotlani bavela njengelizwe elizimeleyo, elizimeleyo kwiXesha eliPhakathi lokuqala kwaye laqhubeka likhona de kwayinkulungwane ye-18. Isizwe sangena kumanyano lobuqu kunye noBukumkani baseNgilani ngo-1603, kulandela ulandelelwano lukaJames VI kuzo zombini iitrone zamaNgesi neScotland. Ngomhla woku-1 kuCanzibe ngo-1707 iScotland yangena ngokusemthethweni kumanyana neNgilani, ngeli xesha ngokwezopolitiko, isenza ilizwe elinye elinegama le-United Kingdom of Great Britain.

I-JAS 39C Gripen yawa endleleni yeenqwelo moya pha ngentsimbi ye 9:30 kusasa kwixesha lasekuhlalenie (0230UTC) yaze yadubula, isikhululo seenqwelo ntaka savalwa ekubeni kufikele kuso iinqwelo ntaka njengesiqhelo. Umqhubi ufumaneke engu Squandron Leader Dilokrit Pattavee Oonondaba basekuhlaleni bxele ukuba isithuthi somlilo sesitishi senqwelomoya siye saphethuka sisazama ukunceda. UVidal woneminyaka eyi-28 wana yinxalenye ye-Barca kwiminyaka emithathu egqithileyo, evela e-Sevilla. Ukusukela kwokuya kwkomkhulu-le-Catalan, u-Vidal idlale imidlalo eyi-49 kweloqela.

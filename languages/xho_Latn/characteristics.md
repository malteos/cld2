# Characteristics (xho)

## Script signals

Click letters `c`, `q`, `x` appear densely inside native roots.
Distinctive multi-letter click clusters: `nc`, `nq`, `nx`, `gc`,
`gq`, `gx`, `ngc`, `ngq`, `ngx`, `ch`, `qh`, `xh`. Xhosa also has
frequent **`hl`** and **`dl`** (more than Zulu), and the trigraphs
`tsh` and `tyh` are characteristic (`utshintsho`, `ityhefu`).

## Top function words

`kwaye` (and), `kodwa` (but), `okanye` (or), `ngenxa yokuba`
(because), `ngoba` (because), `ukuba` (that/if), `xa` (when/if),
`njengoko` (as/since), `nokuba` (even if), `phambi kokuba` (before),
`emva koko` (afterwards), `ke` (then/so), `khona` (there), `apha`
(here), `phaya` (there), `ngoku` (now), `kakhulu` (very), `nokho`
(however), `kananjalo` (moreover), `ndi-` (1sg subject concord —
strong Xhosa marker), `u-` (2sg / class 1), `si-` (1pl), `ba-`
(class 2), `i-` (class 9), `le` / `lo` / `eli` (this), `loo` / `oko`
(that), `ntoni` (what), `bani` (who), `nini` (when), `phi` (where).

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Word-initial augments: `u-`, `a-`, `i-` + class prefixes `um-`,
  `aba-`, `imi-`, `isi-`, `izi-`, `ili-`, `ulu-`, `ubu-`, `uku-`.
- Click trigraphs at onset: `ngc-`, `ngq-`, `ngx-`, `nkc-`, `nkq-`,
  `nkx-`.
- Frequent digraphs: `hl`, `dl`, `ny`, `ng`, `mb`, `nd`, `nj`, `mp`,
  `nk`, `ph`, `th`, `kh`, `bh`, `sh`, `tsh`, `tyh`.
- Verb prefix chains: `ndi-`, `u-`, `si-`, `ba-`, `i-` + TAM
  `ya-`/`za-`/`zo-`/`a-`/`e-` (`ndiyabulela`, `bazokunceda`,
  `siyazi`).
- Final `-ile`, `-anga`, `-ayo` mark perfective, negative past and
  relative.

## Morphology hooks

Productive extensions `-el-`, `-is-`, `-w-`, `-an-`, `-ek-`;
locative circumfix `e-…-eni`; diminutive `-ana`. Negative prefix
`a-` + final `-i`.

## Orthography quirks

Conjunctive spelling keeps clauses as single long words. Clicks and
augments stay lowercase in names (`uMandela`, `iAfrika`). Punctuation
follows English conventions.

# Nigerian Fulfulde LID characteristics

## Script signals

Latin with the hooked implosives **ɓ ɗ ƴ** (U+0253, U+0257, U+01B4) and the velar nasal **ŋ** (U+014B). Presence of **ŋ** combined with **ɓ/ɗ** is a strong Fula signal (Hausa uses the same implosives but not ŋ). Long vowels are written as doubled letters (**aa ee ii oo uu**), and geminate consonants are doubled too (**dd, bb, mm, nn, ll**). No tone marks, no underdots.

## Top function words

**ko**, **no**, **e**, **nde**, **ɗo**, **ɗum**, **oo**, **ɓee**, **ɓe**, **mo**, **ngol**, **ngal**, **nga**, **kam**, **am**, **maa**, **mum**, **makko**, **men**, **amen**, **on**, **ɗon**, **to**, **si**, **njaɓii**, **walaa**, **alaa**, **jooni**, **ngam**, **fii**, **heen**, **ley**, **ɓaawo**, **dow**, **yeeso**, **kala**, **fuu**, **tan**, **bee**, **e nder**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Doubled vowels **aa ee ii oo uu** are ubiquitous, often at stem medial position (*taƴtaa*, *ñalnde*, *seeɗto*).
- Plural ending **-ɓe** (human plural) is a near-unique Fula signature: *fulɓe*, *worɓe*, *rewɓe*.
- Noun-class suffixes **-ngol -ngel -ndu -nde -ngal -nga -ɗo -ɗi -kon -koy** recur at word-final.
- Prenasalised clusters **mb-, nd-, ng-, nj-** at word onset are frequent (*mbaɗɗa*, *ndee*, *njaɓii*).
- Initial consonant mutation pairs: **f/p/b**, **s/c**, **h/k/g**, **w/b/mb**, **y/j/nj**, **r/d/nd** (*fulɓe* ↔ *pullo*, *rewɓe* ↔ *debbo*).

## Morphology hooks

- Verb-person suffix chain **-mi, -ɗaa, -i, -en, -min, -ɗon, -ɓe** appended to a stem is highly diagnostic.
- Voice suffixes **-aade**, **-eede**, **-ude** at word end.
- Negative stems in **-aali**, **-aaki**, **-ataa** are distinctly Fula.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Some traditional writing uses **ɲ** or **ñ** for the palatal nasal; modern standard prefers **ny**. The apostrophe / ʼ appears word-initially in a few forms. Dates and numerals are written in Latin form with doubled vowels (*Wamber*, *ñalnde*).

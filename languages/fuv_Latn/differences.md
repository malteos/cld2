# Fulfulde vs. confusable languages

Nigerian Fulfulde shares hooked letters with Hausa and is close to other Fula varieties (Pulaar fuc, Pular fuf, Maasina ffm). It is not in the CLD2 close-sets, but script overlap with Hausa is the main confusion risk.

## Fulfulde (fuv) vs. Hausa (hau)

- Both use **ɓ ɗ**, but only Fulfulde uses **ŋ** and **ƴ** with any regularity, and only Fulfulde doubles vowels (*aa, ee, ii, oo, uu*).
- Fulfulde has the **-ɓe / -ɗi / -ngol / -ngel / -kon** noun-class suffixes; Hausa has only *-n / -r* linker and masc/fem gender.
- Person is a verb suffix in Fulfulde (*-mi, -ɗaa, -en, -min*); in Hausa it is a pre-verbal PAC clitic (*na, ya, ta, sun, mun*).
- Hausa copula *ne/ce* and **ts-** onset never occur in Fulfulde. Fulfulde focus particle **ko** rarely appears in Hausa.

## Fulfulde (fuv) vs. other Fula varieties (fuc, fuf, ffm)

- fuv is the eastern (Nigerian/Cameroonian) variety; Pulaar/Pular are western (Senegal, Guinea). Lexical differences: fuv *ngoo*, *ñalnde*, *seeɗto* vs. western *ngol*, *ñalawma*, *seeɗto*.
- Western varieties use **ñ**; Nigerian Fulfulde often uses **ny** or **ɲ**.
- Month names (*Seeɗto*, *Wamber*) and place names are the most reliable variety cues.

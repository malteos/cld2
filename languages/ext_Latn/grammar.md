# Extremaduran grammar

- **Word order**: SVO, as in Spanish. Clitic pronouns proclitic to finite verbs, enclitic to non-finite forms.
- **Morphology**: Fusional; close to Spanish but with Astur-Leonese retentions.
- **Nouns**: Two genders M/F plus a residual **neuter** gender used with mass nouns and adjectives (*lo güenu, lo branu*). Number by `-s / -h` (aspirated final). Definite articles Spanish-like `el / la / lu / los / las` but unstressed article often reduced (*l', lu*). Diminutive `-ín/-ina` widely used (*chiquinín, casina*).
- **Adjectives**: Agree in gender/number; postnominal; neuter form in `-u/-o` with mass-noun contexts (*lo branu = "the summer-ness"*).
- **Verbs**: Three conjugations `-al / -el / -il` (note `-l` for `-r` loss: *hablal, corrél, vivil*). Retains preterite vs. compound perfect distinction. Future and conditional on infinitive base. Participles `-áu / -íu` (reduced from `-ado / -ido`).
- **Pronouns**: Pro-drop. Clitics largely Spanish-derived but phonetic reductions: `mi, ti, si, nu, vu` in some varieties.
- **Phonology**: Raising of unstressed final `-o > -u` and `-e > -i` (*hombri, genti, muchu, tiyu*). Aspiration of `-s` (written `-h` in OSCEC: *loh chiquininoh*). Final `-r/-l` merge to `-l` (*comel, cantal*). Initial F- often preserved as aspiration `/h/` — written `j` or `h` (*higu/jigu, hunta/junta*). Diphthongs `ue ie` from Vulgar Latin stressed Ĕ, Ŏ.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; preverbal negation `no`. Use of enclitic `-lu/-la/-lus/-las` with imperatives. Neuter gender `lo X` is a conservative retention not present in Spanish.

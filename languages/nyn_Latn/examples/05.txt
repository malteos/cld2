# Source: https://www.newvision.co.ug/ (New Vision — Uganda news)

KAMPALA - Besweri Kaddu, the father of Dr. Stephen Samuel Kaziimba Mugalu, the Archbishop of the Church of Uganda, is dead . The 93-year-old passed on at Mengo Hospital in Kampala on Tuesday, April 14th. The death was announced by Kaziimba's office in a notice with details of the funeral arrangements.

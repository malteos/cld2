Yoweri Kaguta Museveni Tibuhaburwa (akazarwa 15|9|1944) Ni Omu nya Uganda Kandi omunya eby'obutegyekyi Kandi akahomura okukurira engabo z'eihanga Kandi Ni purezident was mwenda Kandi akiriho nayebembera Purezident wa Uganda. Yoweri Museveni[1] akeetaba omu ntaro nyingi ezayihireho bamwe aha engoma nka Obote na Amin ataka kwatsire bwebembezi omu mwaka 1986 . Museveni aka zaarwa ebiro 15 September 1944 abazeire be Mzee Amos Kaguta (1916–2013), Omutungi w'ente, and Esteri Kokundeka Nganzi (1918–2001), omuri Rukungiri. Ni OmuHima wa eihanga rya Mpororo hati eri emwe Ahari Ankole).

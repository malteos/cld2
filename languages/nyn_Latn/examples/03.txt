Kurigiririra ahari Julius Nyerere, Eishe Museveni Amos Kaguta, akaba Ari omu nyangabo wa King's African Rifles omubwiire bwa orutaro really ensi yoona rwa kabiri . Akaba Ari omu Kakungu ka 7 kabarwani. Museveni kuyaziirwe,abanyabuzaare baguma nibagira ngu Eishe akaba Ari mu-seveni, aho niho yarugirire kutunga eiziina Museveni.

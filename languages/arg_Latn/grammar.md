# Aragonese grammar

- **Word order**: SVO. Clitic pronouns proclitic to finite verbs, enclitic to infinitives and imperatives (*mira-lo, dizir-le*).
- **Morphology**: Fusional; conservative Ibero-Romance. Preserves Latin features lost in Spanish, notably initial F- (*fer, fillo, farina, fuego → fogo/fuego*) and the partitive/locative pronouns `en/ne` and `bi/i` (cognates of Catalan *en, hi* and French *en, y*).
- **Nouns**: Two genders (M/F). Plural in `-s` / `-z` / `-es`. Definite articles `o / a / os / as` (or `lo / la / los / las` in some dialects; `el / la / es / as` in Benasque). This `o/a/os/as` article is one of the strongest Aragonese signals vs. Spanish `el/la/los/las`.
- **Adjectives**: Agree in gender/number; normally postnominal.
- **Verbs**: Three conjugations (`-ar -er -ir`). Rich TAM with present, imperfect, preterite, future, conditional, present/imperfect subjunctive, imperative. Auxiliary `haber` for compound tenses, with some dialects retaining `ser/estar` for unaccusatives. Participle `-ato -ito` (vs. Spanish `-ado -ido`) in conservative varieties.
- **Pronouns**: Pro-drop. Weak clitics `me te se nos tos/vos lo la los las li les en/ne bi/i`.
- **Phonology**: Five-vowel system `a e i o u`. Preserves diphthongs `ue ie` where Spanish also has them but more widely (*fuella, tiengo, ochenta → güeitanta*). Apocope of final vowels in some nouns.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop, preverbal negation `no`. Clitic `en/ne` for partitive/genitive, `bi/i` for locative — shared with Catalan/Occitan but not Spanish.

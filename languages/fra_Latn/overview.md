# French (fra)

## Summary

French (endonym: *français*) is a Romance language in the Indo-European family (Indo-European → Italic → Romance → Gallo-Romance → Oïl). ISO codes: 639-1 `fr`, 639-2 `fre`/`fra`, 639-3 `fra`. It is written in the Latin script and has roughly 80 million native speakers and ~300 million total speakers worldwide. It is the national or co-official language of France, Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Monaco, Canada (esp. Québec), Haiti, and a large number of countries in West and Central Africa (Senegal, Côte d'Ivoire, DRC, Cameroon, etc.), plus overseas French territories.

## Writing system

French uses the 26-letter Latin alphabet with diacritics: acute `é`, grave `à è ù`, circumflex `â ê î ô û`, diaeresis `ë ï ü ÿ`, cedilla `ç`, and the ligatures `œ` and `æ`. Casing is standard bicameral; accents were often omitted on majuscules in older typography but are now recommended. French uses the French-style guillemets `« »` for quotation and inserts a narrow non-breaking space before `; : ! ? » %`.

## Status

French is a co-official UN and EU language and one of the most widely taught L2 languages globally. The Académie française issues normative guidance but is not enforced legally.

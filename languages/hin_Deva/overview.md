# Hindi (hin)

## Summary

Hindi (endonym: **हिन्दी** / **हिंदी** *hindī*) is an Indo-European → Indo-Aryan → Central-Zone language, the Sanskritised register of Hindustani. ISO codes: `hi` (639-1), `hin` (639-2 / 639-3). It is one of the two official languages of the Indian Union and the *de facto* majority tongue of the "Hindi belt" — Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, Uttarakhand and Delhi. Native speakers are estimated at ~340 million; combined with L2 speakers the figure is closer to 600 million, making Hindi the third- or fourth-most-spoken language in the world.

## Writing system

Hindi uses the left-to-right **Devanagari** abugida (Unicode block U+0900–U+097F). Consonants carry an inherent /a/ which can be suppressed by **्** *virāma* (halant) or combined into ligatures. Vowels have independent letters (अ आ इ ई उ ऊ ऋ ए ऐ ओ औ) and dependent "mātrā" signs (ा ि ी ु ू ृ े ै ो ौ). The headline bar **शिरोरेखा** (*shirorekha*) joins letters at the top. Nasalisation is marked by **ं** *anusvāra* or **ँ** *candrabindu*; **ः** *visarga* is rare and mainly in Sanskritic words. Nukta diacritic **़** marks Perso-Arabic sounds (क़, ख़, ग़, ज़, फ़, ड़, ढ़).

## History and status

Modern Standard Hindi crystallised in the late 19th century as a Sanskritised counterpart to Urdu, sharing its spoken base. It is the medium of instruction across the Hindi belt, and the dominant language of Bollywood and mainstream Indian broadcasting.

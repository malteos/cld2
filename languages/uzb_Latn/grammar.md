# Uzbek (Latin) grammar

- **Word order**: Verb-final SOV with modifier–head ordering.
  Order is flexible thanks to case marking.
- **Morphology**: Strongly agglutinative. Unlike most Turkic
  languages, Standard Uzbek has lost systematic vowel harmony —
  suffixes come in single harmonic shapes, which is a
  characteristic that separates Uzbek from Turkish/Azerbaijani
  morphology at a glance.
- **Nouns**: No grammatical gender, no article. Six cases:
  nominative (∅), genitive *-ning*, dative *-ga/-ka/-qa*,
  accusative *-ni*, locative *-da*, ablative *-dan*. Plural
  *-lar*. Possessive suffixes *-im, -ing, -i, -imiz, -ingiz, -lari*
  precede case.
- **Verbs**: Suffix chain: negation *-ma*, ability *-a ol-*,
  tense (*-di* past, *-gan* perfective, *-yap/-yapti* progressive,
  *-adi* present/future, *-moqchi* intentional future, *-ar*
  aorist), person. Copula clitic *-dir*.
- **Phonology**: Six vowels (`a e i o u oʻ`). Lack of front/back
  harmony is a key areal Persianate feature. Consonants include
  /q/ (`q`), /ɣ~ʁ/ (`gʻ`), /ʃ/ (`sh`), /tʃ/ (`ch`), /ŋ/ (`ng`
  digraph). Stress is typically final.
- **Syntax**: Pro-drop. No overt present copula. Negation is
  suffixal. Polar question clitic *-mi*. Participial relatives
  with *-gan*, *-digan*, *-adigan* replace finite relative
  clauses.

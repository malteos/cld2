# Oromo (macro) LID characteristics

## Script signals

ASCII Latin (Qubee) only. No diacritics, no underdots, no hooked letters. Apostrophe **'** marks glottal stop (*har'a*, *ta'uu*, *du'a*). Strongest Oromo fingerprints:

- Double vowels **aa ee ii oo uu** saturate the text.
- Frequent geminate consonants: **dd, mm, nn, ll, rr, ss, tt** (*mootummaa*, *hedduu*, *addunyaa*).
- The digraphs **dh** (implosive) and **ny** (palatal nasal) — rare outside Oromo in Latin text.
- Ejective letter **x** (/tʼ/) inside words (*xiinxala*, *xumura*, *faaxii*).

## Top function words

**fi**, **kan**, **akka**, **ykn**, **ta'ee**, **ta'uu**, **hin**, **dha**, **keessa**, **keessatti**, **irra**, **irraa**, **irratti**, **gara**, **wajjin**, **waliin**, **malee**, **garuu**, **ammoo**, **kanaaf**, **kanaafuu**, **yoo**, **osoo**, **hanga**, **dura**, **booda**, **amma**, **har'a**, **bara**, **waggaa**, **guyyaa**, **tokko**, **lama**, **sadii**, **hedduu**, **baay'ee**, **hunda**, **marti**, **qofa**, **illee**, **iyyuu**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- Word-final `-tti`, `-rraa`, `-iin`, `-dha`, `-uu`, `-aa` (case and nominalising suffixes).
- Preverbal `hin` then a verb ending in `-u`: *hin beeku, hin jiru, hin deemu*.
- The recurrent auxiliary chain **ta'ee**, **ta'uu**, **ta'an**, **ta'a** (copula/become).
- Word-initial `dh-`, `ny-`, `ch-`.
- Numeric/temporal words *waggaa, bara, guyyaa, ji'a, torban* are high-precision cues.

## Morphology hooks

- Verb person suffixes **-e, -te, -ne, -tan, -an** at clause end.
- Noun case suffix **-ni / -n** on subject; **-f** dative; **-iin** instrumental.
- Definite suffix **-icha**, **-ittii**; plural **-oota / -ootni / -oolii**.
- Causative **-siisa**, passive **-ama**, negative imperfective **-u** after **hin**.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Standard Latin punctuation. Apostrophe is load-bearing. Writers frequently use the abbreviation **ykn** ("or", *ykn* = *yookiin*) — an almost unique orthographic fingerprint.

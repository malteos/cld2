# Oromo (macro) grammar

The following description covers common Oromo grammar as written in Qubee; individual variety differences (Borana, Eastern, Orma) are mostly lexical, not structural.

- **Word order**: Strictly **SOV**, head-final, postpositional.
- **Morphology**: Agglutinative and strongly suffixing. Words are built by stringing clean-boundary morphemes onto a root.
- **Nouns**: Gender (masculine/feminine) shown through derivational suffixes and concord. Case suffixes: nominative **-ni / -n**, accusative **-a** (often unmarked), genitive **-(t)ii**, dative **-f**, locative **-tti**, ablative **-irraa**, instrumental **-iin**. Definiteness markers **-icha** (m.), **-ittii / -ttii** (f.).
- **Verbs**: Subject agreement by suffix: 1sg **-e/-a**, 2sg **-tte/-ta**, 3sg.m **-e/-a**, 3sg.f **-te/-ti**, 1pl **-nne/-nna**, 2pl **-ttan**, 3pl **-an**. Main tenses are perfective (**-e**), imperfective (**-a**), and a future formed with an auxiliary (*ni*) + imperfective. Negation uses the preverbal **hin-** with imperfective verbs in **-u**: *hin deemu* "does not go". Productive derivational suffixes: causative **-s / -sis / -siis**, passive **-am**, middle **-at**, reciprocal **-wal-**, iterative reduplication.
- **Phonology**: Five vowels with length contrast, five ejectives (**ch, dh, ph, q, x**), phonemic gemination. No tone. Stress is non-phonemic.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop (person encoded on verb). Copula is the suffix/clitic **-dha / -ttii** on the predicate; existence uses the verb *jira* ("there is") with negator *hin jiru*. Relativisation is marked on the verb itself (*nama deemu* "the man who goes"). The focus particle **-tu** / **-iyyuu** attaches to focused constituents.

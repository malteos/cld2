# Source: Wikipedia article "Fuula Dura3" (https://om.wikipedia.org/)

Muccan tokko kan Aizuwakamatsu, Japan (kabba Tokyo) jirattu achii ka’e hanga “polis xabya” millan demme “sport bag” matta hadha isaa ofii kessa qabu harka polisitti kennu danda’erra. Polisiin akka jettutti, muccaan umrii 17 kuni akka hadha issaa “Monday” galgala kobba ajjesse polisitti himmerra. Achiin bodda matta irra kuttun “sport bag” kessa ka’e polisitti dabarse.

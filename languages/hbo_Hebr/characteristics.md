# Biblical Hebrew LID characteristics

## Script signals

Hebrew block U+0590–U+05FF with heavy **pointing** and **cantillation** —
this is the single most reliable cue separating hbo from heb. Look for:
- Niqqud codepoints U+05B0–U+05BC (ְ ֱ ֲ ֳ ִ ֵ ֶ ַ ָ ֹ ֻ ּ) on virtually every
  consonant.
- Shin/sin dots ׁ ׂ (U+05C1, U+05C2).
- Cantillation marks U+0591–U+05AF (֑ ֖ ֥ ֔ ֛ ֣ ֜ ֙ ֚ ֞ ֧ ֗ ֝ ֠ …) — these
  ornamental accents appear on nearly every word in Masoretic text.
- **Maqqef** ־ (U+05BE) joining short words: אֶת־הָאָרֶץ, בֶּן־דָּוִד.
- **Sof pasuq** ׃ (U+05C3) terminating each biblical verse.
- **Paseq** ׀ (U+05C0) rhythmic divider.

## Top function words

וַ- / וְ- (and/waw-consec), אֲשֶׁר (relative: who/which), אֵת / אֶת- (def. acc.),
כִּי (for/because/that), אִם (if), לֹא (not), אַל (prohibitive not), הִנֵּה
(behold — very diagnostic), וַיְהִי (and it came to pass — signature narrative
opener), וַיֹּאמֶר (and he said — exceedingly frequent), יְהֹוָה (YHWH, Tetragrammaton),
אֱלֹהִים (God), יִשְׂרָאֵל (Israel), בְּנֵי (sons of), בֶּן (son of), בַּת / בַּת־ (daughter),
הָאָרֶץ (the land), הַשָּׁמַיִם (the heavens), לָכֶם / לָהֶם (to you/them pl),
עַל (upon), אֶל (to/toward), מִן / מֵ- (from), עַד (until), לִפְנֵי (before — face of),
אַחֲרֵי (after), תַּחַת (under), בְּתוֹךְ (in the midst of), כֹּה (thus), כֵּן (so).

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- **וַיְ** or **וַיֹּ** starting a word indicates wayyiqtol (narrative past), the
  hallmark Biblical verb form (וַיֹּאמֶר, וַיֵּלֶךְ, וַיְהִי, וַיַּעַשׂ).
- **אֶת־** or **אֵת** immediately before a definite noun: אֶת־הָאָרֶץ, אֵת הַשָּׁמַיִם.
- **אֲשֶׁר** as relative pronoun instead of Modern Hebrew ש-.
- **Maqqef** ־ between short words creates long "compound" orthographic units
  (אֶת־כָּל־בְּנֵי־יִשְׂרָאֵל).
- Verse terminator **׃** at regular intervals.

## Morphology hooks

wayyiqtol prefix **וַיִּ / וַיְ / וַיֵּ / וַתִּ / וָאֶ**. Weqatal וְקָטַל forms.
Cohortative **־ָה** on 1sg (אֶקְטְלָה, נֵלְכָה). Paragogic **־ן** on 2/3pl yiqtol
(יִקְטְלוּן). 2ms possessive pronominal suffix **־ךָ** with qamats (דְּבָרְךָ,
עַבְדְּךָ) — modern Hebrew writes ־ך without niqqud.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Verses numbered with Hebrew letters (א, ב, ג ... כט, ל, לא). Chapters likewise.
Sof pasuq ׃ closes each verse. Paseq ׀, setuma ס, petucha פ as paragraph
markers in Masoretic text.

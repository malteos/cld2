# Standard Malay grammar

- **Word order**: SVO, analytic. Modifier-after-head (`kereta merah`
  "car red" = "red car"). Topic-comment orders frequent in news leads.
- **Morphology**: Mildly agglutinative. Same affix inventory as Indonesian:
  prefixes `meN-`, `di-`, `ber-`, `peN-`, `ter-`, `se-`; suffixes `-kan`,
  `-i`, `-an`; circumfixes `ke-...-an`, `pe(N)-...-an`. Reduplication is
  fully productive with hyphenation in print.
- **Nouns**: No gender, no number, no case. Number via reduplication,
  quantifiers (`banyak`, `beberapa`), or classifiers (`seorang`,
  `sebuah`, `sebatang`, `sekeping`, `seekor`). Possession: `buku saya`,
  `bukunya`.
- **Verbs**: No person/tense agreement. Aspect/mood by particles `sudah`,
  `sedang`, `akan`, `belum`, `pernah`, `masih`. Voice distinctions active
  `meN-` / patient-focus `di-` / accidental `ter-`. Imperatives often
  bare or with `-lah` (`duduklah`).
- **Phonology**: Six vowels; final stops often unreleased; penultimate
  stress. Native inventory lacks /f v z/; English loans retain them.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop common. Zero copula in present. `ialah` for
  equative clauses ("X is Y"), `adalah` for attributive/locative.
  Relativiser `yang`; negation `tidak`/`tak` for predicates, `bukan` for
  nominal negation, `belum` for "not yet". Question particle `-kah`
  (`Sihatkah awak?`).

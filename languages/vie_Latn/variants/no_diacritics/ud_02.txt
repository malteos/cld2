Doan nao kho di thi da co nguoi dung cho tu truoc de lay. Neu mu tre con thi ban nhu cho. Dot hang nay gia tri khong cao boi gia kha beo, hon nua lai cong kenh. Nhung chiec dau dia CD, VCD... chat cao 5 - 6 m, trai rong hang tram met vuong. Rieng viec hoc dai hoc tuy thieu chinh xac mot chut (vi dang hoc chu khong phai da tot nghiep), nhung co the thong cam. Tuy nhien, voi 253 km duong bien, chung toi khong the chan het duoc... Nguoi mua thi khong quan tam hang nhap lau hay noi dia. Dem ra vuon cat thu, thay suong tay, anh o ngoai vuon cat tia suot ngay quen ca com nuoc. Tho ve. Gia dinh Tho lai troi theo cuoc song day lo toan, ban ron. Hai y tu noi: la thang con trai muon the hien suc manh thi can tranh bi coi thuong, nhat la ve dau oc. Dang tro chuyen, chot mot thanh nien trong doan khach het toang: "O, tuan, may sang day lam gi?". May lan di qua Buoi vao nha tim nhung bo me may noi may di lam xa... Tung noi khong nen voi vang ma me phai am tham tim hieu. Nhung tuyen duong khong co nha dan thi cuu van khan truong nhay len xe may lao ve diem tap ket.

# Vietnamese (vie_Latn) - Grammar Sketch

## Typology

Vietnamese is an **isolating, analytic** language. Words never inflect for case, number, gender, person or tense; grammatical information is expressed by **word order**, **function words** and **particles**. Basic order is **SVO**, modifiers follow the head (noun + adjective, noun + demonstrative), and the language is **topic-prominent** and freely omits subjects and objects recoverable from context. Most morphemes are a single syllable; words are often compounds of two (or more) syllables, written with spaces between syllables: *học sinh* "pupil" (study + produce), *máy bay* "airplane" (machine + fly).

## Phonology

- **Syllable structure**: (C)(w)V(C) + tone. Codas are restricted to *-p, -t, -c/-ch, -m, -n, -ng/-nh* and the glides *-i/-y, -o/-u*. No consonant clusters within the onset (spelled digraphs such as *ch, nh, ng, th, tr, kh, ph, gi, qu* each stand for one sound or a sound + glide).
- **Tones** (Northern standard, six), marked on the main vowel:

| Tone | Mark | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ngang (level) | none | *ma* "ghost" |
| huyền (falling) | grave ` | *mà* "but" |
| sắc (rising) | acute ´ | *má* "mother (S.)/cheek" |
| hỏi (dipping) | hook above ̉ | *mả* "grave" |
| ngã (broken rising) | tilde ˜ | *mã* "horse (S-V)/code" |
| nặng (heavy) | dot below ̣ | *mạ* "rice seedling" |

  Syllables ending in *-p, -t, -c, -ch* take only *sắc* or *nặng* (*một, học, đẹp, việc*).
- **Vowels**: *a, ă, â, e, ê, i/y, o, ô, ơ, u, ư* plus diphthongs/triphthongs *ia/iê, ua/uô, ưa/ươ, oai, uyê, ươi* etc. (*nguyên, người, chuyện*).

## Nouns and classifiers

Nouns are bare: *sách* "book/books". Plurality is shown, when needed, by pre-nominal markers *những, các* ("the various", "all the"), or numerals. Counting and specific reference require a **classifier**: *con* (animals, some objects), *cái* (inanimate things), *cuốn/quyển* (books), *chiếc* (vehicles, single items), *người* (people), *bức, tờ, quả, ngôi*:

- *hai con mèo* "two cats", *cái bàn này* "this table", *những chiếc xe đó* "those vehicles".

Noun phrase order: [quantifier] [plural marker] [classifier] **noun** [adjective] [possessor] [demonstrative]: *ba cuốn sách mới của tôi* "my three new books"; *ngôi nhà lớn này* "this big house". Possession uses *của* "of" (*nhà của bạn* "your house") or simple juxtaposition (*mẹ tôi* "my mother").

## Pronouns and address

Personal reference relies heavily on **kinship terms** chosen by relative age and status: *anh* (older brother / young man / "you"), *chị* (older sister), *em* (younger sibling), *ông* (grandfather / Mr.), *bà* (grandmother / Mrs.), *cô, chú, bác, con, cháu*. Neutral or other pronouns: *tôi* "I", *bạn* "you (friend)", *nó* "he/she/it (familiar)", *mình* "self/I/we", *ta* "we/I". Plural is formed with *chúng* or *các*: *chúng tôi* "we (excl.)", *chúng ta* "we (incl.)", *các bạn* "you (pl.)", *họ* "they".

## Verbs and aspect

Verbs do not inflect. Time and aspect are marked by pre-verbal particles, or left to context:

- *đã* (past/anterior), *đang* (progressive), *sẽ* (future), *vừa/mới* (just), *sắp* (about to), *rồi* (already, clause-final), *chưa* (not yet; question tag).
- *Tôi đã ăn rồi.* "I have already eaten." *Anh ấy đang làm việc.* "He is working." *Mai chúng tôi sẽ đi.* "Tomorrow we will go."

Negation: *không* (general), *chưa* (not yet), *chẳng/chả* (emphatic, informal), *đừng* (prohibitive): *Tôi không biết.* "I don't know."

Copula *là* links nouns: *Đây là sách.* "This is a book." Adjectives act as predicates without a copula: *Cô ấy đẹp.* "She is beautiful." Existence/possession uses *có*: *Có nhiều người.* "There are many people."

**Passive** is formed with *bị* (adverse) or *được* (beneficial): *Anh ấy bị phạt.* "He was punished." *Cô được khen.* "She was praised." *được* also means "can / get to" after a verb (*làm được*), and *có thể* means "can" before it.

**Serial verbs** are common: *đi mua sắm* "go shopping", *mang về* "bring back".

## Syntax

- Yes/no questions: *có ... không?* (*Anh có khỏe không?* "Are you well?"), or final particles *à, hả, chứ, nhỉ*.
- Wh-words stay in place: *Anh đi đâu?* "Where are you going?" *Cái này là gì?* "What is this?"; *ai* "who", *nào* "which", *sao/tại sao* "why", *bao nhiêu* "how many".
- Relative clauses follow the noun, optionally with *mà*: *người mà tôi gặp* "the person whom I met".
- Conjunctions: *và* "and", *nhưng* "but", *hoặc/hay* "or", *vì* "because", *nên* "so", *nếu ... thì* "if ... then", *khi* "when", *để* "in order to", *rằng* "that".
- Prepositions: *trong* "in", *trên* "on", *ở/tại* "at", *với* "with", *cho* "for/give", *từ* "from", *đến/tới* "to", *về* "about/back".
- Sentence-final particles convey attitude and politeness: *ạ* (polite), *nhé/nha* (softening), *đi* (urging), *mà* (insistence).

## Short examples

- *Tôi là người Việt Nam.* "I am Vietnamese."
- *Hôm qua chúng tôi không đi học vì trời mưa.* "Yesterday we didn't go to school because it rained."
- *Những người này đang chờ ở ngoài.* "These people are waiting outside."

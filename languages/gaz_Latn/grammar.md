# West Central Oromo grammar

- **Word order**: **SOV** head-final. Modifiers precede the head; postpositions, not prepositions (*mana keessa* "inside the house"). Verb is strictly clause-final in unmarked clauses.
- **Morphology**: Highly **agglutinative/suffixing**. Nouns and verbs string together multiple clear-boundary suffixes.
- **Nouns**: Two genders (masculine / feminine), often indicated by the final vowel or by a derivational suffix (*-ttii*, *-eessa* feminine). Case is marked on the noun: nominative **-ni / -n** (*namni*, *nama → namni*), accusative **-a** (often zero), genitive **-(t)ii**, dative/locative **-f**, ablative **-irraa**, instrumental **-n**. Definite/demonstrative **-cha**, **-ttii**, **-icha**, **-ittii**.
- **Verbs**: Person and number marked by suffix: 1sg **-e/-a**, 2sg **-tte/-ta**, 3sg.m **-e/-a**, 3sg.f **-te/-ti**, 1pl **-nne/-nna**, 2pl **-ttan**, 3pl **-an**. Tense/aspect: perfective **-e**, imperfective **-a**, future **-a + ni/n**. Negation uses the proclitic/prefix **hin-** / **hin…u** (*hin deemu* "does not go"). Causative **-s-/-sis-**, passive **-am-**, reciprocal **-wal-**.
- **Phonology**: Five vowels with contrastive length (*a/aa, e/ee, i/ii, o/oo, u/uu*). Five ejectives written **ch, dh, ph, q, x** in Qubee. Gemination is phonemic and frequent. No tone.
- **Syntax notes**: Pro-drop; subject is recoverable from the verb suffix. Copula **-(d)ha** suffixes to predicates ("Oromoo-dha" = "he is an Oromo"). Relativisation marked by suffix **-u / -an** on the verb. Focus particle **-tu / -iyyu** ("even").

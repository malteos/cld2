# West Central Oromo LID characteristics

## Script signals

Pure ASCII Latin (Qubee). No diacritics, no hooked letters, no underdots. The apostrophe **'** marks glottal stop and appears word-internally (*du'a*, *har'a*, *ta'uu*). Highly distinctive features:

- Double vowels **aa ee ii oo uu** in almost every content word (*afaan*, *oromoo*, *seeraa*, *irraa*).
- Double consonants (gemination) everywhere: **dd, gg, kk, ll, mm, nn, pp, rr, ss, tt** (*hedduu*, *addunyaa*, *dhugaa*, *mootummaa*).
- The digraph **dh** (implosive) is extremely common, as is **ny**; both are near-exclusive to Oromo/Borana in Latin CC/Web text.

## Top function words

**fi**, **kan**, **akka**, **ykn**, **ta'ee**, **ta'uu**, **hin**, **dha**, **dhaa**, **keessa**, **irra**, **irraa**, **irratti**, **gara**, **wajjin**, **waliin**, **malee**, **garuu**, **ammoo**, **kanaaf**, **kanaafuu**, **kanaan**, **yoo**, **otoo**, **osoo**, **hanga**, **dura**, **booda**, **ammaas**, **amma**, **har'a**, **bara**, **waggaa**, **guyyaa**, **tokko**, **lama**, **hedduu**, **baay'ee**, **hunda**, **marti**.

## N-gram / spelling patterns

- `-tti`, `-rra`, `-rraa`, `-iin` suffix clusters at word end (case markers).
- `ta'` appears as a bound auxiliary: *ta'ee, ta'uu, ta'an*.
- Word endings **-uu**, **-aa**, **-oo** on infinitives and nominals; **-cha**, **-ttii** on definite forms.
- Sequences `dh-`, `ch-`, `ny-` word-initial are diagnostic (*dhufe, dhaabbata, chaappaa, nyaata*).
- `hin-` prefix before a verb signals negation (*hin jiru*, *hin beeku*).

## Morphology hooks

- Verb person endings **-e, -te, -ne, -tan, -an** at sentence end.
- Case ending **-n / -ni** on subject nouns, **-f** dative, **-irraa** ablative, **-iin** instrumental.
- Copula clitic **-dha / -ttii** on predicative nouns.

## Punctuation / orthography quirks

Apostrophe is content-bearing (glottal stop). Writers occasionally use **'y** for palatal glottal in loanwords. No ASCII digits in place of letters. The word **Afaan** (literally "mouth/language") in a text is an almost certain Oromo cue.

# Guadeloupean Creole French (gcf)

ISO 639-3: gcf | Family: French-based creole | Region: Guadeloupe | Script: Latin

## Confused with
- **Haitian Creole (hat)**: 15/24 misclassified. Both Antillean French-based creoles.

## Distinctive features vs Haitian Creole

| Feature | Guadeloupean (gcf) | Haitian (hat) |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| Orthography | **"gy"/"ky"**: gyèl, kyè | "dj"/"tj" or absent |
| Possessive | **"a-w"** (your), **"a-y"** (his) | "ou" / "li" |
| Modal "must" | **"fò"**: "fò-w alé" | **"fòk"**: "fòk ou ale" |
| Demonstrative | **"lasa"** / **"sala"** | **"sa a"** |
| Conjunction | **"kè"** | **"ke"** / **"ki"** |
| Article | Uniform **"la"** for all nouns | Variable by phonology |
| Story formula | **"krik krak"** | **"krik? krak!"** |

## Key vocabulary (unique quadgrams)
- gy/ky sequences: gyèl, kyè, gyé, kyanm
- Possessive: a-w, a-y, a-nou (your, his, our)
- Modal: fò-w, fò-y, fò-nou (you must, he must, we must)
- Breton-derived: chivrèt (crayfish), krik krak
- Spanish-derived: matadò (traditional dress)
- Topics: gwoka (drum music), kassav (cassava bread), karnaval

## Example sentences
- "Sa ou fé? Mwen byen, mèsi." (How are you? I'm fine, thanks.)
- "Fò-w alé adan kaz a-w." (You must go to your house.)
- "Sé moun la ka dansé gwoka." (The people are dancing gwoka.)
- "Krik krak! An ké rakonté a-w on bèl istwa." (Krik krak! I will tell you a beautiful story.)

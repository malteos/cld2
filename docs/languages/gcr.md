# Guianese Creole French (gcr)

ISO 639-3: gcr | Family: French-based creole | Region: French Guiana | Script: Latin

## Confused with
- **Unknown (und)**: 77/111. Most quadgrams not distinctive enough to match any language.
- **Haitian Creole (hat)**: 22/111.

## Distinctive features vs Haitian Creole

| Feature | Guianese (gcr) | Haitian (hat) |
|---------|---------------|---------------|
| Possessive | **Preposed**: "mo kaz" (my house) | Postposed: "kay mwen" |
| 1st person | **"mo"** | **"mwen"** |
| 2nd person | **"to"** | **"ou"** |
| 3rd person | **"so"** / **"yé"** | **"li"** |
| Progressive | **"ka"**: "mo ka manjé" | **"ap"**: "mwen ap manje" |
| Plural | **"-yan"**: "moun-yan" | **"yo"**: "moun yo" |
| Onset /r/ | Preserved: "rivyè" | Replaced by /w/: "rivyè" |
| Reduplication | Productive: "dodo-dodo" | Limited |

## Key vocabulary (unique quadgrams)
- mo ka, to ka, so ka, yé ka (progressive constructions)
- mo kaz, to fanm, so pitit (preposed possessives)
- -yan (plural marker)
- paské (because), atò (then), dèyè (behind)
- Arawak loans: words from Amerindian substrate

## Example sentences
- "Mo ka alé dan mo kaz." (I am going to my house.)
- "To ka manjé épi so fanm." (You are eating with his wife.)
- "Sé moun-yan ka travay paské yé ka bezwen lajan." (The people work because they need money.)
